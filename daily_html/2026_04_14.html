Identifying Inductive Biases for Robot Co-Design

Abstract (English): Co-designing a robot's morphology and control can ensure synergistic interactions between them, prevalent in biological organisms. However, co-design is a high-dimensional search problem. To make this search tractable, we need a systematic method for identifying inductive biases tailored to its structure. In this paper, we analyze co-design landscapes for soft locomotion and manipulation tasks and identify three patterns that are consistent across regions of their co-design spaces. We observe that within regions of co-design space, quality varies along a low-dimensional manifold. Higher-quality regions exhibit variations spread across more dimensions, while tightly coupling morphology and control. We leverage these insights to devise an efficient co-design algorithm. Since the precise instantiation of this structure varies across tasks and is not known a priori, our algorithm infers it from information gathered during search and adapts to each task's specific structure. This yields $36\%$ more improvement than benchmark algorithms. Moreover, our algorithm achieved more than two orders of magnitude in sample efficiency compared to these benchmark algorithms, demonstrating the effectiveness of leveraging inductive biases to co-design.

摘要（中文）: 协同设计机器人的形态与控制可以确保它们之间的协同交互，这在生物有机体中普遍存在。然而，协同设计是一个高维搜索问题。为了让这个搜索变得可处理，我们需要一种系统性的方法来识别针对其结构的归纳偏置。本文分析了软体运动和操作任务的协同设计景观，并识别出了在其协同设计空间各个区域中一致的三种模式。我们观察到，在协同设计空间的区域内，质量沿着低维流形变化。高质量区域表现出分布在更多维度上的变化，同时形态与控制之间存在紧密耦合。我们利用这些见解来设计一种高效的协同设计算法。由于这种结构的具体实现因任务而异，且事先未知，我们的算法从搜索过程中收集信息并进行推断，以适应每个任务的具体结构。这比基准算法多获得了36%的改进。此外，我们的算法与这些基准算法相比实现了超过两个数量级的样本效率提升，展示了利用归纳偏置进行协同设计的有效性。

TLDR: The paper proposes a method to uncover task‑specific inductive biases in robot co‑design by analyzing co‑design landscapes, showing that high‑quality designs lie on low‑dimensional manifolds, and uses these insights to build an efficient algorithm that yields 36% performance gains and over 100× better sample efficiency.
