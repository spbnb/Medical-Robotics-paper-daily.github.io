InnerLogic-Surg: An Open Surgical Foundation Model via Continual Pretraining

Abstract (English): Vision foundation models are the backbone of modern surgical image analysis, yet most surgical systems still rely on encoders pretrained on natural images or on specialist models trained from scratch on comparatively small datasets. Pretraining encoders on large amounts of surgery-specific data has the potential to generally enhance the performance of surgical image analysis methods simply by providing stronger backbones. Here we present InnerLogic-Surg, an open family of surgical vision encoders (21M--300M parameters) obtained by continually pretraining a transformer-based image encoder on millions of surgical images curated from 11,965 internet-sourced surgical videos spanning ~2,900 hours and more than fifteen procedure types. Our training recipe inherits DINOv3's pretraining stage to preserve representation ability after surgical adaptation, and additionally applies a learned feature-upsampling module for high-resolution dense prediction at low token cost. Across a benchmark covering workflow, instrument and anatomy recognition, segmentation, detection, and monocular depth, under the same architecture, the largest InnerLogic-Surg model achieves the best linear-probe performance in all 20 surgical tasks. We demonstrate that surgical continual pretraining yields transferable representations that generalize across procedures, anatomical regions, and task types, showing our surgical image encoders as a promising and scalable alternative to the procedure-specific representation learning approaches that have traditionally dominated surgical computer vision.

Computation, not weight access: large language models as auditors of clinical prediction networks

Abstract (English): Frontier large language models (LLMs) have been reported to read, edit and write the weights of small neural networks, raising the prospect of auditing clinical prediction models from their files alone. Public demonstrations, however, mixed reading raw weights with running code. We separated these conditions in internally pre-specified simulation experiments. Tanh networks of 105 to 38,401 weights were trained on simulated cohorts of patients with subarachnoid haemorrhage, each with a planted relationship or none, and one network was trained on labels that under-recorded deaths in a subgroup. GPT-6 Astra and four other GPT models answered single prompts under a default or an explicit system instruction, either with the weights in the prompt and no tools, or with a calculation-only tool that exposed the weights (white box) or only the network's predictions (black box). Without tools, Astra recovered planted relationships in 353-weight networks but became unreliable at 3,457 weights, and under the default instruction it declined 11 of 12 audit and repair tasks as unidentifiable; the explicit instruction removed these refusals. With the tool, it recovered the planted relationship in all 29 runs on networks of up to 38,401 weights, raised no false alarm in 15 runs on null networks, and found the biased subgroup in 6 of 6 runs. Black-box access performed as well as white-box access, and other GPT-6 models read as accurately. A well-designed script matched the comparator models on the audit and the best LLM repair. For auditing these networks, what mattered was computation and elicitation, not access to the weights.

摘要（中文）: 前沿大型语言模型（LLM）已被报道能够读取、编辑和编写小型神经网络的权重，这引发了仅从临床预测模型文件本身即可对其进行审计的前景。然而，公开演示往往将读取原始权重与运行代码混在一起。我们在内部预先指定的模拟实验中分离了这些条件。我们训练了包含105至38,401个权重的Tanh网络，所用数据集为模拟的蛛网膜下腔出血患者队列，每个网络要么带有预设的关联关系，要么不带任何关联关系；此外，还有一个网络在某个亚组的死亡记录被低估的标签上进行了训练。GPT-6 Astra及其他四个GPT模型在默认或明确系统指令下回答单一提示，要么在提示中直接提供权重且不使用工具，要么使用一个仅用于计算的工具，该工具或暴露权重（白盒），或仅暴露网络的预测结果（黑盒）。在没有工具的情况下，Astra能够恢复含353个权重的网络中预设的关联关系，但在含有3,457个权重的网络上变得不可靠；在默认指令下，它拒绝了12项审计与修复任务中的11项，声称无法识别；而明确指令消除了这些拒绝。使用工具后，Astra在包含多达38,401个权重的网络上的全部29次运行中均恢复了预设关联关系，在15次针对无效网络的运行中未产生任何误报，并在全部6次运行中发现了存在偏倚的亚组。黑盒访问的表现与白盒访问相当，其他GPT-6模型也达到了同等的读取准确度。一个精心设计的脚本在审计任务上与对照模型相当，在修复任务上则与最佳LLM相当。对于这些网络的审计而言，重要的是计算与信息引导，而非对权重的访问权限。

TLDR: This paper tests whether large language models can audit clinical prediction networks by accessing model weights or by using computation/prediction queries. In simulated clinical cohorts, LLMs with a calculation tool accurately recovered planted relationships and biases without needing weight access, showing computation and elicitation matter more than raw weight visibility.

简要说明: 本文检验大型语言模型能否通过读取模型权重或通过计算/预测查询来审计临床预测网络。在模拟临床队列中，配备计算工具的LLM无需访问权重即可准确恢复预设关系和偏倚，表明计算与提示方式比权重可见性更为关键。

Reclassification Is All You Missed: Surfacing Variant Reinterpretation Need in Online Health Communities at Population Scale

Abstract (English): Genetic variants are routinely reclassified in ClinVar after a patient is tested, sometimes from uncertain to pathogenic, yet patient recontact is inconsistent, with no clear duty and responsibility diffused among labs, providers, and patients. Many affected people discuss their results in public online health communities that clinical recontact often does not reach. We built PRISM, an agentic large-language-model pipeline that screens these posts, checks whether the variant was later reclassified, and drafts a disclaimer-bearing outreach message, in a no-contact dry run. Across 3.9 million posts from 32 communities, agent-predicted reinterpretation-relevant discussion is rare (0.288%) but concentrated in specialist genetics communities (6.30% versus 0.10% in general medical-advice communities) and growing fivefold since 2014; PRISM surfaces 123 candidate classification differences, 5 of them from an uncertain submission to pathogenic or likely pathogenic. On a held-out set of 30 real-post drafts, a clinician judged 27 factually grounded and 15 fit to send: factual accuracy is necessary but not sufficient for clinically acceptable outreach. PRISM contributes a population-scale reinterpretation-need map with code and data to be released upon publication.

摘要（中文）: 遗传变异常常在患者接受检测后被常规地重新分类于ClinVar数据库中，有时从“意义不明确”变为“致病性”，然而对患者的再联系并不一致，没有明确的责任，且义务分散在实验室、医疗服务提供者和患者之间。许多受影响的个体在公共在线健康社区中讨论他们的检测结果，而这些社区往往是临床再联系所触及不到的。我们构建了PRISM，一种代理式大语言模型流水线，用于筛选这些帖子，检查相关变异之后是否被重新分类，并起草带有免责声明的外联信息，进行无接触的试运行。在来自32个社区的390万条帖子中，代理预测的与重新解释相关的讨论很罕见（0.288%），但集中于专业遗传学社区（6.30%，而一般医疗建议社区为0.10%），且自2014年以来增长了五倍；PRISM发现了123个候选的分类差异，其中5个是从“意义不明确”提交变为“致病性”或“可能致病性”。在一组30篇真实帖子草稿的保留测试集上，一位临床医生判定27篇事实依据充分，15篇适合发送：事实准确性是临床上可接受的外联的必要但不充分条件。PRISM提供了一张人群规模的重新解释需求图谱，代码和数据将在发表时发布。

TLDR: PRISM is an agentic LLM pipeline that scans 3.9 million online health community posts to detect patient-discussed genetic variants that were later reclassified in ClinVar, then drafts disclaimer-bearing outreach messages in a no-contact dry run. It finds rare but concentrated and growing reinterpretation needs, surfacing 123 candidate classification differences including 5 with prognostic shifts from uncertain to pathogenic or likely pathogenic.

CardioLM: A multilingual suite of small language models for the cardiology domain

Abstract (English): Background: Cardiovascular care generates large volumes of clinical free-text documentation in local languages. However, clinical natural language processing (NLP) resources remain concentrated in English, and no existing benchmark is cardiology-specific, multilingual, and broad in entity coverage. Methods: We present two contributions. The first is CardioCCC, a corpus of 508 cardiology case reports originally published in Spanish and extended to Czech, Dutch, English, Italian, Romanian, and Swedish through machine translation, annotation projection, and clinician review, with annotations covering diseases, symptoms, procedures, and medications. The second is CardioLM, comprising CardioBERTa, a set of language-specific encoders obtained by continued pretraining on cardiology and biomedical corpora, and CardioNER, a set of named entity recognition (NER) models fine-tuned on CardioCCC. Results: Cardiology-specific pretraining reduced pseudo-perplexity by up to a factor of eight, yet changed strict span-level F1 by at most 0.04, with benefits concentrated in languages whose backbones lacked biomedical coverage. The jointly trained multilingual XLM-RoBERTa-large models achieved the best strict F1 in nearly every language and entity type, exceeding the strongest monolingual system by up to 0.13 F1. A large share of residual errors concerned boundary detection: relaxed matching recovered 0.10-0.23 F1 for disease, procedure, and symptom, but only 0.01-0.07 for medication. Clinicians rated CardioNER well above a rule- and dictionary-based baseline, with the mean rating across entity types rising from 26% to 73%. Conclusions: Specialty-specific pretraining is not the decisive factor for downstream entity recognition, whereas multilingual joint training yields the strongest results in nearly all settings and a simpler deployment path for multi-country clinical information extraction.

摘要（中文）: 摘要： 背景：心血管护理会产生大量以本地语言书写的临床自由文本记录。然而，临床自然语言处理（NLP）资源仍主要集中在英语，且现有基准数据集没有一个是针对心脏病学、多语言且实体覆盖广泛的。 方法：我们贡献了两项成果。第一是CardioCCC，一个包含508篇心脏病学病例报告的语料库，这些报告最初以西班牙语发表，并通过机器翻译、标注投影和临床医生审核扩展至捷克语、荷兰语、英语、意大利语、罗马尼亚语和瑞典语，标注覆盖疾病、症状、手术和药物。第二是CardioLM，包含CardioBERTa——一组通过对心脏病学和生物医学语料库进行持续预训练而获得的语言特定编码器，以及CardioNER——一组在CardioCCC上微调得到的命名实体识别（NER）模型。 结果：针对心脏病学的预训练将伪困惑度最多降低了八倍，但对严格跨度级F1值的影响最多仅为0.04，且收益主要集中在基础模型缺乏生物医学覆盖的语言中。联合训练的多语言XLM-RoBERTa-large模型在几乎所有语言和实体类型上取得了最佳严格F1值，比最强单语系统高出最多0.13个F1点。很大一部分剩余错误与边界检测有关：宽松匹配使疾病、手术和症状的F1值恢复了0.10–0.23，但药物仅恢复了0.01–0.07。临床医生对CardioNER的评分远高于基于规则和词典的基线系统，各实体类型的平均评分从26%升至73%。 结论：专科特定的预训练并非下游实体识别的决定性因素，而多语言联合训练在几乎所有场景下都取得了最强结果，并为多国临床信息提取提供了更简单的部署途径。

TLDR: This paper introduces CardioCCC, a multilingual cardiology case-report corpus with clinician-reviewed annotations, and CardioLM, a suite of encoder/NER models; it finds multilingual joint training outperforms cardiology-specific continued pretraining for NER.
