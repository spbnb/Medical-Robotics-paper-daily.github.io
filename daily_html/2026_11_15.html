Modeling the Frictional Contact of Continuum Robots with Environments through Linear Complementarity Problem

Abstract (English): Accurate friction modeling in continuum robots is essential for precise motion control and path planning. However, current approaches make predictions only for the mechanism's internal friction that is limited by the boundary forces of the Coulomb friction cone, which fail to characterize the static friction and motion hysteresis. In this paper, we propose a novel friction model based on complementarity constraints under the Coulomb friction assumption. This model predicts various friction effects caused by environmental interactions, including both static and sliding frictions and hysteresis behavior, as well as the corresponding robot deformation induced by these interactions, which are crucial for the practical motion planning and control of continuum robots. Additionally, real-time implementation of this model can be achieved, meeting the demand for computational efficiency in robotic applications. Experimental validations demonstrated that this model is able to reduce the maximum tip position error from 45.4 mm in the frictionless model to 5.85 mm in our friction-included model with a 287.9 mm-long tendon-driven continuum robot.

摘要（中文）: 连续体机器人的精确摩擦建模对于精确运动控制和路径规划至关重要。然而，现有方法仅能对机构内部摩擦进行预测，且受限于库仑摩擦锥的边界力，无法表征静摩擦和运动滞后。本文提出一种基于库仑摩擦假设下互补约束的新型摩擦模型。该模型能够预测由环境交互引起的各种摩擦效应，包括静摩擦、滑动摩擦及滞后行为，以及这些交互作用导致的机器人相应变形，这对连续体机器人的实际运动规划和控制至关重要。此外，该模型可实现实时运行，满足机器人应用中对计算效率的需求。实验验证表明，该模型能够在长度为287.9毫米的腱驱动连续体机器人上，将最大末端位置误差从无摩擦模型的45.4毫米降至含摩擦模型的5.85毫米。

TLDR: This paper proposes a friction model for continuum robots based on linear complementarity constraints under Coulomb friction, capturing static/sliding friction and hysteresis caused by environmental contact. Experiments on a 287.9 mm tendon-driven continuum robot reduce maximum tip position error from 45.4 mm (frictionless) to 5.85 mm (with friction).

Agentic induction of common data elements and information extraction from sarcoma case report abstracts

Abstract (English): Common data elements (CDEs) support clinical data comparability but are usually defined manually. We investigated whether large language model agents could derive a CDE schema from sarcoma case report abstracts and apply it at scale. An agent proposed 72,909 candidate CDEs from 24,588 PubMed abstracts; agentic consolidation yielded 1,570 CDEs. We retained 53 supported by at least 50 abstracts and attempted evidence-linked extraction across the corpus. Processing succeeded for all retained CDEs in 79.2% of abstracts, including responses indicating non-mention. Two human reviewers approved 83.0% of retained CDEs for relevance, 67.9% for clarity, and 56.6% for correctness. In 100 sampled abstracts, exact agreement with the value extracted by the LLM was 94.9% among 1,116 pairs with a non-empty answer (abstract-level bootstrap 95% CI, 93.0-96.4%). Errors concentrated in anatomical and temporal fields. These findings support agent-assisted schema drafting followed by expert curation before large-scale extraction.

摘要（中文）: 常用数据元（CDEs）支持临床数据的可比性，但通常需人工定义。我们研究了大型语言模型智能体能否从肉瘤病例报告摘要中推导出CDE模式，并将其规模化应用。智能体从24,588篇PubMed摘要中提出了72,909个候选CDE；经智能体整合后得到1,570个CDE。我们保留了至少被50篇摘要支持的53个CDE，并尝试在整个文献库中进行基于证据链接的提取。在79.2%的摘要中，所有保留的CDE均成功完成处理，包括表明未提及的响应。两位人工评审员分别对保留CDE的相关性、清晰度和正确性给出了83.0%、67.9%和56.6%的批准率。在100篇抽样摘要中，与LLM提取值完全一致的比例为94.9%（基于1,116对非空答案，摘要层面自举95%置信区间为93.0-96.4%）。错误主要集中在解剖学和时间相关字段。这些发现支持在规模化提取之前，先进行智能体辅助的模式草拟，再经过专家审核。

TLDR: This paper proposes an LLM-agent pipeline that derives a common data element (CDE) schema from 24,588 sarcoma case report abstracts, then applies it for evidence-linked information extraction at scale. With expert curation, 53 retained CDEs achieved high extraction agreement (94.9%) but limited per-CDE correctness, suggesting agents can assist schema drafting rather than fully automate it.

简要说明: 本文提出一种大语言模型智能体流水线，从24,588篇肉瘤病例报告摘要中归纳常见数据元（CDE）模式并大规模提取证据链接数据。经专家筛选后，53个保留CDE的提取一致性较高（94.9%），但CDE级别正确率有限，表明智能体更适合辅助模式起草而非完全自动化。
