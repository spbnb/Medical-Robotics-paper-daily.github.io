Right Diagnosis, Unsafe Route: Auditing Clinical AI Decision Trajectories and Corrective Oversight for Validity and Potential Harm

Abstract (English): A clinical AI system can reach a correct final diagnosis through intermediate decisions that could have harmed the patient, and can repair an error while introducing new risks. Most evaluations score final diagnoses; even stepwise benchmarks, which release a case's evidence over time, score whether systems reach the right diagnosis or gather the right information, not whether the decisions a patient passes through are safe. We make patient safety the target of stepwise evaluation. A trajectory audit replays real admissions checkpoint by checkpoint, lets each system decide with only the evidence released so far, and rates every test, treatment and disposition for its potential harm to the patient and, separately, for clinical validity, against the documented final diagnosis and physician-written disease rubrics yet on the evidence available when the decision was made. An LLM judge scores every decision, and a physician reference standard (triple annotation and senior adjudication of a stratified sample) calibrates the rates. An oversight audit branches each trajectory at its first actionable error and measures, on the same branches, whether corrective feedback repairs the error and whether it introduces new harm. Across 300 cases from a hospital cardiology cohort, MIMIC-IV and the public ASCENT benchmark, with up to seven AI systems per source, 55.2% of hospital trajectories reaching an acceptable final diagnosis passed through a decision with moderate or severe potential harm and 24.8% through a clinically unacceptable one; the harm rates were 76.2% on MIMIC-IV and 39.6% on ASCENT. Structured corrections resolved 73.6% of first errors versus 24.8% after bare rejection, yet new potentially unsafe decisions followed 30.2% of corrections versus 19.7% of rejections. A correct final diagnosis does not certify a safe trajectory, and a successful correction does not certify a safe recovery.

摘要（中文）: 摘要：一个临床AI系统可以通过一系列本可能伤害患者的中间决策得出正确的最终诊断，并且可能在修复一个错误的同时引入新的风险。大多数评估只对最终诊断评分；即使是逐步基准测试（按时间顺序逐步发布病例证据），评分的也是系统是否得出正确诊断或收集了正确信息，而非患者所经历的决策是否安全。我们将患者安全作为逐步评估的目标。轨迹审计逐检查点回放真实入院过程，让每个系统仅依据当时已发布的证据进行决策，并针对记录的最终诊断和医师撰写的疾病标准，对每项检查、治疗和处置分别评估其对患者的潜在伤害以及临床有效性——且仅基于决策作出时已有的证据。一个LLM法官对每项决策进行评分，一个医生参考标准（对分层样本进行三重标注和高级裁决）对评分率进行校准。监督审计在每条轨迹的首次可操作错误处分叉，并在相同的分叉上衡量纠正性反馈是否修复了该错误以及是否引入了新的伤害。在医院心脏病学队列、MIMIC-IV和公共ASCENT基准的300个病例中（每个来源最多七个AI系统），55.2%达到可接受最终诊断的医院轨迹经历了一个具有中度或重度潜在伤害的决策，24.8%经历了临床不可接受的决策；伤害率在MIMIC-IV上为76.2%，在ASCENT上为39.6%。结构化纠正解决了73.6%的首次错误，而单纯拒绝后仅为24.8%，但纠正后出现了30.2%的新的潜在不安全决策，而拒绝后这一比例为19.7%。正确的最终诊断并不能保证轨迹安全，成功的纠正也不能保证安全的恢复。

TLDR: This paper proposes trajectory and oversight audits for clinical AI systems, scoring the safety and validity of every intermediate decision rather than only the final diagnosis, and shows that correct final diagnoses and successful corrections can still conceal harmful decisions or introduce new risks.

Not All Evidence Is Admissible: Evidence Governance for Tool-Augmented Medical Agents

Abstract (English): Tool-augmented medical agents treat whatever their tools return as evidence. We show that this default is costly: medical tools fail silently, and a frozen reasoner cannot ignore what enters its context, so many cases it would have answered correctly are flipped to wrong once tool outputs are added. We call this evidence hijacking. $\mathcal{V}$-information explains the phenomenon: the reasoner has lost the option to ignore, so a tool output can carry negative usable information, and moving the decision of what to keep outside the reasoner restores that option. The theory further shows that part of any filtering gain comes merely from letting the reasoner see less, and only selection by content lets it see better. Building on this, we recast tool-augmented reasoning as evidence governance, which separates deciding what counts as evidence from the reasoner that answers, and propose context-independent evidence governance (CIEG), the first medical agent framework that rules, after acquisition, on whether each individual tool output may enter as evidence. Every tool output is written as a card that can be checked against the raw input; an independent checker that sees only the input and the cards decides which cards become evidence; and the reasoner answers from the admitted cards alone, with no training anywhere. On medical question answering, fundus glaucoma screening, and chest X-ray pneumonia classification, it achieves the highest accuracy among models of comparable scale, and significantly outperforms state-of-the-art tool-using agents and multi-agent systems built on the same backbone at lower cost.

摘要（中文）: 摘要： 工具增强的医学智能体将工具返回的所有内容都视为证据。我们发现这一默认做法代价高昂：医学工具会悄然失效，而冻结的推理器无法忽略进入其上下文的信息，因此许多原本能够正确回答的病例，在加入工具输出后反而被错误回答。我们将这种现象称为“证据劫持”。$\mathcal{V}$-信息解释了这一现象：推理器失去了忽略的选项，因此工具输出可能携带负的可利用信息，而将保留哪些信息的决策移出推理器则恢复了该选项。理论进一步表明，任何过滤收益的一部分仅仅来自于让推理器看到更少的内容，只有基于内容的筛选才能让它看得更好。在此基础上，我们将工具增强推理重新诠释为“证据治理”，它将“什么算作证据”的决策与负责回答的推理器分离，并提出了上下文无关证据治理（CIEG）——首个在获取工具输出后、对每个单独的工具输出是否可作为证据进入进行裁决的医学智能体框架。每个工具输出都被写成一张卡片，可与原始输入进行核对；一个仅查看输入和卡片的独立检查器决定哪些卡片成为证据；推理器仅依据被接纳的卡片进行回答，全程无需训练。在医学问答、眼底青光眼筛查和胸部X光肺炎分类任务中，它在同规模模型中取得了最高准确率，并以更低成本显著优于基于相同主干构建的最先进的工具使用智能体和多智能体系统。

TLDR: This paper identifies a failure mode in tool-augmented medical agents where tool outputs can mislead a fixed reasoner, and proposes evidence governance with a context-independent checker to admit only useful tool outputs as evidence, improving medical QA and imaging classification without additional training.

Unsupervised Multi-modal Medical Image Registration via Invertible Translation

Abstract (English): In medical imaging, the alignment of multi-modal images plays a critical role in providing comprehensive information for image-guided therapies. Despite its importance, multi-modal image registration poses significant challenges due to the complex and often unknown spatial relationships between different image modalities. To address this, we introduce a novel unsupervised translation-based multi-modal registration method, termed Invertible Neural Network-based Registration (INNReg). INNReg consists of an image-to-image translation network that converts multi-modal images into mono-modal counterparts and a registration network that uses the translated mono-modal images to align the multi-modal images. Specifically, to ensure the preservation of geometric consistency after image translation, we introduce an Invertible Neural Network (INN) that leverages a dynamic depthwise convolution-based local attention mechanism. Additionally, we design a novel barrier loss function based on Normalized Mutual Information to impose constraints on the registration network, which enhances the registration accuracy. The superior performance of INNReg is demonstrated through experiments on two public multi-modal medical image datasets, including MRI T1/T2 and MRI/CT pairs. The code is available at https://github.com/MeggieGuo/INNReg

OICycle: Observe, Infer, and Intervene Cycle for Belief-State Learning in Longitudinal Clinical Decision Making

Abstract (English): Clinical care is a closed loop: clinicians observe partial evidence, infer the patient's state, and intervene, and each intervention determines what is observed next. This makes clinical decision making a partially observable Markov decision process (POMDP), in which a belief over a latent patient state must be updated from irregular, multimodal, and incomplete records. Yet most deep clinical models predict from a single snapshot or from coarsely aggregated history, while POMDP-based clinical methods need hand-specified discrete state spaces and transition models that real electronic health records cannot supply. We introduce OICycle (Observe, Infer, and Intervene Cycle), which learns a stochastic belief state directly from retrospective trajectories. At every episode, a learned recurrent operator fuses a sample of the previous belief with the new multimodal observations and the intervention that produced them. The resulting Gaussian belief is trained to predict clinicians' next interventions, the time to the next episode, and next-episode mortality. To enable reproducible evaluation, we release a benchmark derived from MIMIC-IV, MIMIC-IV-Note, and MIMIC-CXR that organizes 72,075 hospital admissions (5.4M laboratory, microbiology, radiology, prescription, and note records) into temporally ordered therapeutic--diagnostic episodes. Compared with an otherwise identical history-free model, OICycle improves next-intervention adaptive recall from 0.253 to 0.347, time-to-next-episode macro-F1 from 0.293 to 0.351, and next-episode mortality macro-F1 from 0.585 to 0.649. Analysis shows that the learned belief encodes intervention type and trajectory position and evolves smoothly across episodes, and that the benefit of history is concentrated in interventions whose ordering depends on prior findings.

摘要（中文）: 临床护理是一个闭环：临床医生观察部分证据，推断患者状态，并进行干预，而每次干预又决定了接下来观察到的内容。这使得临床决策成为一个部分可观测马尔可夫决策过程（POMDP），其中对潜在患者状态的信念必须从不规则、多模态且不完整的记录中更新。然而，大多数深度临床模型仅从单一快照或粗略聚合的历史记录进行预测，而基于POMDP的临床方法则需要人工指定的离散状态空间和转移模型，这些是真实电子健康记录无法提供的。我们提出OICycle（观察、推断与干预循环），该方法直接从回顾性轨迹中学习随机信念状态。在每个回合中，一个学习得到的循环算子将先前信念的样本与新的多模态观测以及产生这些观测的干预相融合。由此得到的高斯信念被训练用于预测临床医生的下一次干预、到下一回合的时间以及下一回合的死亡率。为实现可复现的评估，我们发布了一个源自MIMIC-IV、MIMIC-IV-Note和MIMIC-CXR的基准数据集，将72,075次住院记录（540万条实验室、微生物学、放射学、处方和文本记录）组织为按时间排序的治疗-诊断回合。与否则完全相同的无历史模型相比，OICycle将下一次干预的自适应召回率从0.253提升至0.347，到下一回合时间的宏F1从0.293提升至0.351，下一回合死亡率的宏F1从0.585提升至0.649。分析表明，学习得到的信念编码了干预类型和轨迹位置，并在各回合之间平滑演化，且历史信息的收益主要集中在干预顺序依赖于先前发现的干预中。

TLDR: OICycle learns a stochastic belief state over latent patient state from longitudinal EHR trajectories using a recurrent POMDP-inspired operator, and shows improved prediction of clinicians' next interventions, time-to-next-episode, and mortality on a large MIMIC-based benchmark.

SimAI: Mechanistic User Simulation through Psychological Network Models

Abstract (English): Realistic user simulation enables the study of human interactions when direct experimentation is costly or potentially harmful. However, existing simulators largely overlook the psychological mechanisms that drive user behavior. We introduce SimAI, a framework for mechanistic user simulation that models how psychological factors interact to shape user responses. To overcome the challenge of supervising open-ended behavior, we propose a graph reconstruction objective that provides graph-level rewards based on how well simulated conversations reflect the underlying psychological relationships. To support this approach, we construct PSYCHE, a dataset of 18,576 profile-network pairs derived from 516 psychotherapy cases. Automatic evaluations show that SimAI achieves greater simulation and behavioral diversity than existing patient simulators while maintaining strong profile alignment. In an interactive study with 30 mental-health professionals and trainees, SimAI receives the highest average ratings for conversational authenticity, realistic help-seeking behavior, and clinical training utility. Beyond simulation quality, SimAI enables realistic clinical training and uncovers safety failures in prolonged AI interactions with vulnerable users. Code is available at https://github.com/behavioral-data/ANGEL-UserSim.

摘要（中文）: 真实用户模拟使得在直接实验成本高昂或可能造成伤害时，能够研究人类交互行为。然而，现有模拟器在很大程度上忽略了驱动用户行为的心理机制。我们提出SimAI，一个用于机制性用户模拟的框架，该框架建模心理因素如何相互作用以塑造用户回应。为克服开放行为监督的挑战，我们提出一种图重构目标，该目标基于模拟对话在多大程度上反映潜在心理关系来提供图级奖励。为支持该方法，我们构建了PSYCHE数据集，该数据集包含源自516例心理治疗案例的18，576个档案-网络对。自动评估表明，SimAI在保持较强档案对齐的同时，相较于现有患者模拟器实现了更高的模拟多样性和行为多样性。在一项包含30名心理健康专业人员及受训者的互动研究中，SimAI在对话真实性、求助行为逼真度和临床训练实用性方面获得了最高平均评分。除模拟质量外，SimAI还实现了逼真的临床训练，并揭示了AI与弱势用户长期交互中的安全隐患。代码可在https://github.com/behavioral-data/ANGEL-UserSim获取。

Authors: Cheng Li, Adhyyan Narang, Jiayi Yuan, John F. Buss, Jina Suh, Devon Sandel-Fernandez, Yulia Tsvetkov, Adam M. Kuczynski, Tim Althoff

Visual Harness Scales General VLMs to Medical Images

Abstract (English): We show that an appropriate visual harness can substantially improve the performance of general vision-language models (VLMs) across diverse medical imaging tasks. We introduce VERA, a medical visual harness that equips general VLMs with interactive visual tools and systematic diagnostic evidence management. With VERA, general VLMs interactively explore medical images and progressively acquire diagnostic evidence, which is preserved in a lossless memory for subsequent retrieval and use. After submitting an initial answer, the VLM receives an assessment of potential omissions and the reliability of identified findings. This assessment guides further image inspection and measurement, followed by answer revision in an iterative agentic loop until sufficient evidence is obtained. Across 11 tasks and four imaging modalities, VERA improves performance on all tasks with Qwen3.8-27B, with notable gains in spleen measurement (63.3% to 98.3%), renal search (57.5% to 71.2%), and pulmonary nodule detection (0.0% to 28.2%). High-capability API-based VLMs achieve even higher performance with VERA. For example, GPT-6.1 Sol improves pulmonary nodule detection from 10.8% to 69.2% with VERA, demonstrating the complementary benefits of stronger model capabilities and an effective medical visual harness. Our results highlight the potential of visual harnesses as a new paradigm for scaling general VLMs to medical imaging tasks.

摘要（中文）: 我们证明，适当的视觉“护栏”（visual harness）能够显著提升通用视觉-语言模型（VLM）在多种医学影像任务中的表现。我们引入了VERA——一种医学视觉护栏，为通用VLM配备交互式视觉工具和系统化的诊断证据管理。借助VERA，通用VLM能够交互式地探索医学图像并逐步获取诊断证据，这些证据以无损记忆形式保存，供后续检索和使用。在提交初始答案后，VLM会收到关于潜在遗漏和已识别发现可靠性的评估。该评估引导进一步的图像检查和测量，随后在迭代的智能体循环中修正答案，直至获得充分证据。在11项任务和四种影像模态中，VERA在Qwen3.8-27B模型上提升了所有任务的表现，其中在脾脏测量（63.3%提升至98.3%）、肾脏搜索（57.5%提升至71.2%）和肺结节检测（0.0%提升至28.2%）方面增益显著。基于API的高能力VLM在VERA的辅助下取得了更高性能。例如，GPT-6.1 Sol借助VERA将肺结节检测从10.8%提升至69.2%，展示了更强的模型能力与有效医学视觉护栏之间的互补优势。我们的结果凸显了视觉护栏作为将通用VLM扩展到医学影像任务的新范式的潜力。

Authors: Shaowen Wan, Hanqi Jiang, Zihao Wu, Junhao Chen, Weihang You, Ruiyu Yan, Bowen Chen, Tianming Liu, Lin Zhao

Beyond Acuity Accuracy: A Multidimensional Framework for Evaluating Patient-Facing Emergency Triage AI Tools

Abstract (English): Patient-facing AI triage tools may support care-seeking and emergency department prioritization, but require evaluation beyond acuity accuracy alone. We developed SAFE-Triage, a framework integrating human review, automated metrics, LLM-as-judge evaluation, and simulation testing across clinical performance, patient interaction, and clinician-facing summary quality. We applied SAFE-Triage to 40 MIMIC-IV-derived emergency vignettes and a 10,030-case acuity analysis comparing prompted LLM triage (PLT) with rule-augmented prompted LLM triage (rPLT).
