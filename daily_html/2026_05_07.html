Autonomous Laparoscope Control through Unified Mechanics-Based Representation of Multimodal Intraoperative Information

Abstract (English): Laparoscope-holding robots can provide surgeons with a stable laparoscopic field of view (FOV) and reduce the burden on human assistants. To maintain an ideal intraoperative FOV, the robot must continuously adjust the laparoscope pose according to intraoperative information. However, intraoperative multimodal signals, such as position, force/torque, and images, differ markedly in physical meaning and units, making it difficult to build a unified representation and to generate control commands that can be used directly for laparoscope control. To address this issue, we propose a laparoscope-holding robot control method based on unified mechanics modeling of multimodal information. First, we design mapping strategies for multiple intraoperative sources, including position, force/torque, and images, and unify them into an equivalent-wrench representation in the operational space. Then, using a task-priority scheme, we inject the wrenches into the task space and the null space, respectively, and synthesize laparoscope control commands via task-priority projection, thereby achieving consistent representation and coordinated fusion of multimodal information within a single framework. Finally, taking the intraoperative remote center of motion (RCM) position, force/torque sensor readings, and laparoscopic images as examples, we construct an RCM-constraint wrench to enforce the RCM geometric constraint and reduce the contact force at the trocar site, a laparoscope-manipulation wrench to enable compliant dragging, and an instrument-tracking wrench to achieve autonomous visual tracking of the instruments. Experiments on a surgical phantom and in vivo porcine trials demonstrate that the proposed method supports multi-task operation, including compliant laparoscope manipulation and autonomous instrument tracking, while maintaining the RCM constraint and reducing sustained trocar-site loading.

摘要（中文）: 腹腔镜持镜机器人能够为外科医生提供稳定的术中视野，并减轻人力辅助负担。为维持理想的手术视野，机器人需根据术中信息持续调整腹腔镜位姿。然而，术中位置、力/力矩、图像等多模态信号在物理意义与量纲上差异显著，难以构建统一表征并生成可直接用于腹腔镜控制的指令。为此，本文提出一种基于多模态信息统一力学建模的腹腔镜持镜机器人控制方法。首先，针对位置、力/力矩及图像等多源术中信息设计映射策略，将其统一表达为操作空间中的等效力旋量；随后采用任务优先级策略，将力旋量分别注入任务空间与零空间，通过任务优先级投影合成腹腔镜控制指令，从而在统一框架内实现多模态信息的一致表征与协调融合。最后，以术中远程运动中心位置、力/力矩传感器读数及腹腔镜图像为例，分别构建用于强化远程运动中心几何约束并降低套管接触力的约束力旋量、实现顺应性拖动的操作力旋量，以及完成手术器械自主视觉跟踪的追踪力旋量。在手术仿体与活体猪实验中，本方法在保持远程运动中心约束、降低持续套管负载的同时，成功实现了包含顺应性腹腔镜操控与自主器械跟踪在内的多任务操作。
