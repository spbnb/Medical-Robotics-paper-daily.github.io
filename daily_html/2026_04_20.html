HyKey: Hyperspectral Keypoint Detection and Matching in Minimally Invasive Surgery

Abstract (English): Purpose: 3D reconstruction in minimally invasive surgery (MIS) enables enhanced surgical guidance through improved visualisation, tool tracking, and augmented reality. However, traditional RGB-based keypoint detection and matching pipelines struggle with surgical challenges, such as poor texture and complex illumination. We investigate whether using snapshot hyperspectral imaging (HSI) can provide improved results on keypoint detection and matching surgical scenes. Methods: We developed HyKey, a HYperspectral KEYpoint detection and description model made up of a hybrid 3D-2D convolutional neural network that jointly extracts spatial-spectral features from HSI. The model was trained using synthetic homographic augmentation and epipolar geometry constraints on a robotically-acquired dual-camera RGB-HSI laparoscopic dataset of ex-vivo organs with calibrated camera poses. We benchmarked performance against established RGB-based methods, including SuperPoint and ALIKE. Results: Our HSI-based model outperformed RGB baselines on registered RGB frames, achieving 96.62% mean matching accuracy and 67.18% mean average accuracy at 10 degree on pose estimation, demonstrating consistent improvements across multiple evaluation metrics. Conclusion: Integrating spectral information from an HSI cube offers a promising approach for robust monocular 3D reconstruction in MIS, addressing limitations of texture-poor surgical environments through enhanced spectral-spatial feature discrimination. Our model and dataset are available at https://github.com/alexsaikia/HyKey-Hyperspectral-Keypoint-Detection

摘要（中文）: 目的：3D重建在微创手术（MIS）中能够通过改善可视化、工具跟踪和增强现实来增强手术指导。然而，传统的基于RGB的关键点检测和匹配流程在处理手术挑战时存在困难，例如纹理缺失和复杂光照。我们研究是否使用快照式高光谱成像（HSI）能够在关键点检测和手术场景匹配方面提供更好的结果。方法：我们开发了HyKey，一个高光谱关键点检测与描述模型，由混合3D-2D卷积神经网络组成，能够从HSI中联合提取空间-光谱特征。该模型使用合成单应性增强和极线几何约束进行训练，基于机器人获取的离体器官双摄像头RGB-HSI腹腔镜数据集，并配备已校准的相机姿态。我们与成熟的RGB方法（包括SuperPoint和ALIKE）进行了性能基准测试。结果：我们的基于HSI的模型在已注册的RGB帧上优于RGB基线方法，实现了96.62%的平均匹配精度和67.18%的平均精度（在姿态估计的10度阈值下），并在多个评估指标上展现出持续的性能提升。结论：整合来自HSI立方体的光谱信息为微创手术中的稳健单目3D重建提供了一种有前景的方法，通过增强光谱-空间特征区分能力来解决纹理匮乏的手术环境限制。我们的模型和数据集可在https://github.com/alexsaikia/HyKey-Hyperspectral-Keypoint-Detection获取。
