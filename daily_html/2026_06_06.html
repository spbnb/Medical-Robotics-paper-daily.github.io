MedSIGHT: Towards Grounded Visual Comprehension in Medical Large Vision-Language Models

Abstract (English): Medical large vision-language models (Med-LVLMs) have recently achieved remarkable progress in vision-language comprehension and medical image segmentation. However, existing models still struggle to unify these two capabilities, which is essential for achieving clinically reasoning that connects visual findings with semantic interpretation. We present MedSIGHT, a unified framework that equips Med-LVLMs with structured, pixel-level understanding for grounded visual comprehension. MedSIGHT introduces a novel Region Perceiver module that produces region-centric tokens, encoding spatial information directly into representation space of the language model. We further propose a medical region codebook into the LLM vocabulary, allowing the model to generate discrete region codes as symbolic representations of anatomical and pathological regions. These codes are decoded through the Region Perceiver to reconstruct segmentation mask, achieving end-to-end spatial grounding. Lastly, MedSIGHT combines Region Perceiver, Codebook and LLM using our proposed progressive training strategy to gradually aligns these modules stably. Trained on only 72K multimodal instruction pairs, MedSIGHT achieves state-of-the-art performance across diverse imaging modalities on both medical comprehension and segmentation tasks.

摘要（中文）: 医学大视觉语言模型（Med-LVLMs）近期在视觉语言理解与医学图像分割领域取得了显著进展。然而，现有模型仍难以将这两种能力统一起来，而这对于实现连接视觉发现与语义解释的临床推理至关重要。我们提出MedSIGHT——一个统一框架，赋予Med-LVLMs结构化、像素级的理解能力，以实现有依据的视觉理解。MedSIGHT引入了一个新型区域感知器模块，该模块生成以区域为中心的标记，将空间信息直接编码到语言模型的表征空间中。我们进一步在LLM词汇表中引入了一个医学区域码本，使模型能够生成离散的区域代码，作为解剖和病理区域的符号表示。这些代码通过区域感知器解码以重建分割掩码，从而实现端到端的空间定位。最后，MedSIGHT采用我们提出的渐进训练策略，将区域感知器、码本与LLM相结合，逐步稳定地对齐这些模块。仅使用72K多模态指令对进行训练，MedSIGHT在多种成像模态的医学理解与分割任务中均达到了最先进性能。

TLDR: MedSIGHT introduces a unified framework for medical large vision-language models that integrates region-level spatial understanding and segmentation via a Region Perceiver module and a medical region codebook, achieving state-of-the-art performance on both comprehension and segmentation tasks across modalities with only 72K training pairs.

LLM-Guided Evolution for Medical Decision Pipelines

Abstract (English): Adapting large language models (LLMs) to clinical workflows often requires costly fine-tuning or manual prompt and pipeline engineering. We study LLM-guided MAP-Elites evolution as an inference-time alternative for discovering medical decision strategies and provide an implementation repository at https://github.com/univanxx/llm_guided_evo_medical. We formulate urgency triage, interactive consultation, and medical image classification as evolutionary searches over executable artifacts optimized by task-specific fitness functions. Across all three settings, evolution improves over manually designed baselines under practical constraints. In triage, evolved programs increase Semigran accuracy from $77.3\%$ to $87.1\%$ and emergency recall from $0.60$ to $0.97$, while improving safety-weighted held-out MIMIC-ESI performance. In interactive consultation, evolved policies improve the accuracy--cost frontier across Llama-3, Qwen-3.5, and Gemma-4 and transfer to held-out iCRAFTMD. In PneumoniaMNIST, prompt-only evolution improves frozen MedGemma VLMs while preserving strict JSON outputs. Qualitative analysis shows that the gains come from interpretable program-level mechanisms, calibrated triage boundaries, targeted evidence acquisition, selective commitment, and finding-oriented visual decision rules, rather than superficial prompt rewording alone.

摘要（中文）: 将大型语言模型（LLMs）适配到临床工作流程通常需要昂贵的微调或手动提示词与流程工程。我们研究LLM引导的MAP-Elites演化作为一种推理时替代方案，用于发现医疗决策策略，并在https://github.com/univanxx/llm_guided_evo_medical提供实现仓库。我们将紧急分诊、交互式咨询和医学图像分类形式化为对可执行工件的演化搜索，这些工件由任务特定的适应度函数优化。在所有三种设置中，演化在实践约束下均优于手动设计的基线。在分诊中，演化程序将Semigran准确率从77.3%提升至87.1%，紧急召回率从0.60提升至0.97，同时改善了安全加权留出MIMIC-ESI性能。在交互式咨询中，演化策略优化了Llama-3、Qwen-3.5和Gemma-4的准确率-成本前沿，并迁移至留出的iCRAFTMD。在PneumoniaMNIST中，仅提示词演化改进了冻结的MedGemma VLM，同时保持严格的JSON输出。定性分析表明，性能提升源于可解释的程序级机制、校准的分诊边界、目标化证据获取、选择性承诺以及面向发现的视觉决策规则，而非仅靠表面的提示词改写。

TLDR: This paper proposes using LLM-guided MAP-Elites evolution as an inference-time method to automatically discover medical decision strategies for triage, interactive consultation, and medical image classification, achieving significant performance gains over manually designed baselines.

Compliance-Based Sensor Placement for Force Sensing on a Sensorized Prostate Phantom

Abstract (English): This work presents a compliance-based sensor placement method for force sensing on a sensorized prostate phantom designed for Digital Rectal Examination training. The phantom combines three internal pneumatic chambers, used as intrinsic pressure sensors, with ten surface displacement markers. A finite-element simulation dataset is generated by applying external forces at sampled surface locations, from which a compliance matrix relating force inputs to pressure and displacement responses is constructed. Based on this matrix, we propose a weighted greedy selection strategy that maximizes local force reconstructability while prioritizing the clinically relevant posterior contact region and avoiding marker placement directly within the Region of Interest. Compared with a global QR-based placement strategy, the proposed method increases the mean reconstructability score in the target region by 22.5%. These results suggest that region-aware sparse sensor placement can improve force observability in soft robotic medical phantoms while maintaining a limited and practical sensing configuration.

摘要（中文）: 本工作提出了一种基于柔度的传感器布置方法，用于专为直肠指检训练设计的传感器化前列腺体模的力感知。该体模将三个用作内部压力传感器的气动腔室与十个表面位移标记相结合。通过在采样表面位置施加外力生成有限元仿真数据集，由此构建了关联力输入与压力及位移响应的柔度矩阵。基于该矩阵，我们提出了一种加权贪婪选择策略，该策略在最大化局部力可重构性的同时，优先考虑临床相关的后接触区域，并避免将标记直接布置在感兴趣区域内。与基于全局QR分解的布置策略相比，所提方法将目标区域的平均可重构性评分提高了22.5%。这些结果表明，区域感知的稀疏传感器布置能够在保持有限且实用的感知配置的同时，提升软体机器人医疗体模中的力可观测性。

TLDR: This paper presents a region-aware, compliance-based sparse sensor placement method for a sensorized prostate phantom used in Digital Rectal Examination training, achieving a 22.5% improvement in force reconstructability over a global QR-based strategy.

简要说明: 本文提出了一种基于顺应性的区域感知稀疏传感器布局方法，用于数字直肠检查训练中的传感前列腺模型，相较于全局QR策略，力重建性能提升了22.5%。
