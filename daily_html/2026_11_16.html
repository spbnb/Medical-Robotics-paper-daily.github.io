EndoComparer: Comparative Surgical Video Question Answering with Pseudo-Siamese Visual Adaptation and Perceive-then-Compare Reasoning

Abstract (English): Purpose: Surgical training benefits from explicit comparison between cases: in patient condition, in technique and in skill proficiency. Surgical vision-language models (VLMs) offer a promising and flexible interface for this purpose. Yet current VLMs typically see one video at a time, and they often fall short when asked to compare videos. Methods: We propose EndoComparer, a VLM for comparative surgical video question answering with two components. (a) A Pseudo-Siamese Visual LoRA (PSVL) gives each video slot its own low-rank adapter over a shared frozen vision encoder, separating the two videos in the visual pathway at the cost of a single encoder pass. (b) A Perceive-then-Compare chain of thought (PtC-CoT) trains the model to first state what each video shows, then derive the answer from these statements. We further curate EndoDiffVQA, a benchmark of nearly 11K multiple-choice questions on video-pair over cholecystectomy and gastric bypass procedures, covering anatomy, tool, action and skill. Results: EndoComparer outperforms strong generalist and surgical VLMs on detecting and specifying difference between videos. Ablations show that two proposed components contribute complimentarily, and the model remains more robust under a video-swap control that exposes confusion between the two videos. Conclusion: Providing a dedicated visual reasoning path through PSVL and PtC-CoT makes cross-case comparison of surgical videos more robust for VLMs. EndoDiffVQA and EndoComparer provide a benchmark and a strong baseline for this task. Project page: https://camma-public.github.io/endocomparer

摘要（中文）: 摘要： 目的：外科手术培训受益于病例之间的显式比较：包括患者状况、技术和技能熟练程度。手术视觉语言模型（VLM）为此提供了一种有前景且灵活的界面。然而，当前的VLM通常一次只处理一个视频，在要求比较视频时往往表现不足。方法：我们提出了EndoComparer，一种用于比较性手术视频问答的视觉语言模型，包含两个组件。(a) 伪孪生视觉LoRA（PSVL）在共享的冻结视觉编码器上为每个视频槽分配其自己的低秩适配器，在单次编码器遍历的成本下，在视觉通路中将两个视频分离。(b) 先感知后比较的思维链（PtC-CoT）训练模型首先陈述每个视频所展示的内容，然后从这些陈述中推导出答案。我们还构建了EndoDiffVQA，这是一个包含近11K个关于胆囊切除术和胃旁路手术视频对的多项选择题基准，涵盖解剖、工具、动作和技能。结果：EndoComparer在检测和描述视频间差异方面优于强大的通用型和手术型VLM。消融实验表明，两个提出的组件具有互补贡献，并且在视频交换控制下模型保持更强的鲁棒性，该控制暴露了两个视频之间的混淆。结论：通过PSVL和PtC-CoT提供专用的视觉推理路径，使VLM对外科手术视频的跨病例比较更加稳健。EndoDiffVQA和EndoComparer为此任务提供了基准和强基线。项目页面：https://camma-public.github.io/endocomparer

TLDR: EndoComparer is a surgical vision-language model for comparative video QA, introducing a Pseudo-Siamese visual LoRA to process video pairs with one encoder and a Perceive-then-Compare chain-of-thought to improve cross-video reasoning, along with a new 11K-question benchmark for cholecystectomy and gastric bypass comparisons.

简要说明: EndoComparer 是一种面向手术视频对比问答的视觉语言模型，通过伪孪生视觉 LoRA 在共享编码器下处理视频对，并采用“先感知后比较”的推理链提升跨视频比较能力，同时构建了包含约 11K 个问题的内镜手术对比问答基准。
