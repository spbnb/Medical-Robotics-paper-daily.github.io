ForceFlow: Learning to Feel and Act via Contact-Driven Flow Matching

Abstract (English): Existing imitation learning methods enable robots to interact autonomously with the physical environment. However, contact-rich manipulation tasks remain a significant challenge due to complex contact dynamics that demand high-precision force feedback and control. Although recent efforts have attempted to integrate force/torque sensing into policies, how to build a simple yet effective framework that achieves robust generalization under multimodal observations remains an open question. In this paper, we propose ForceFlow, a force-aware reactive framework built upon flow matching. For contact-stage policy design, we investigate force signal fusion mechanisms and adopt an asymmetric multimodal fusion architecture that treats force as a global regulatory signal, combined with a joint prediction paradigm that enhances the policy's understanding of instantaneous force and historical information, thereby achieving deep coupling between force and motion. For task-level hierarchical decomposition, we divide manipulation into a vision-dominant approach stage (VLM-based pointing for target localization) and a touch-dominant interaction stage (force-driven contact execution), with a Vision-to-Force (V2F) handover mechanism that explicitly decouples spatial generalization from contact regulation. Experimental results across six real-world contact-rich tasks demonstrate that ForceFlow achieves a 37% success rate improvement over the strong baseline ForceVLA while maintaining significantly lower cost. Moreover, ForceFlow exhibits accurate force signal prediction and demonstrates superior performance in contact force self-regulation and zero-shot out-of-distribution (OOD) generalization.

摘要（中文）: 现有模仿学习方法使机器人能够自主与物理环境交互。然而，由于接触动力学复杂且需要高精度力反馈与控制，接触密集型操作任务仍面临重大挑战。尽管近期研究尝试将力/力矩传感融入策略模型，但如何构建简单有效的框架以实现多模态观测下的鲁棒泛化仍是一个开放问题。本文提出ForceFlow——一种基于流匹配的力感知反应框架。针对接触阶段的策略设计，我们研究了力信号融合机制，采用非对称多模态融合架构，将力作为全局调控信号，并结合联合预测范式以增强策略对瞬时力与历史信息的理解，从而实现了力与运动的深度耦合。在任务级分层分解方面，我们将操作任务划分为视觉主导的接近阶段（基于VLM的指向目标定位）与触觉主导的交互阶段（力驱动的接触执行），通过视觉到力量（V2F）交接机制显式解耦空间泛化与接触调控。在六项真实世界接触密集型任务中的实验结果表明，ForceFlow在保持显著更低成本的同时，比强基线方法ForceVLA实现了37%的成功率提升。此外，ForceFlow展现出精确的力信号预测能力，并在接触力自调节与零样本分布外泛化方面表现出卓越性能。

TLDR: ForceFlow is a framework for contact-rich robotic manipulation that uses force as a global regulatory signal within a flow matching policy, and employs a two-stage hierarchical architecture (vision-based approach followed by force-driven interaction) to decouple and improve generalization.

简要说明: ForceFlow 是一个用于接触密集型机器人操作的框架，它通过在流匹配策略中将力作为全局调节信号，并采用两阶段分层架构（基于视觉的接近和基于力的接触）来解耦任务并提升泛化能力。
