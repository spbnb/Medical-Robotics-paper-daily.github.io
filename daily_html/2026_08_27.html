THA-Flow Generative Model: Prosthesis Geometry Prediction from Preoperative CT

Abstract (English): Preoperative planning for total hip arthroplasty (THA) is commonly framed as selecting a single prosthesis configuration and placement for a patient's osseous anatomy. In practice, however, the same anatomy may admit several clinically reasonable solutions, making planning inherently a one-to-many problem that is better represented by a conditional probability distribution. We present THA-Flow, a conditional flow-matching model that generates three-dimensional prosthesis geometry directly from preoperative CT. Separate AutoencoderKL models compress preoperative bone anatomy and prosthesis geometry, while a three-dimensional UNet learns a rectified flow from Gaussian noise to the prosthesis latent space under spatial bone conditioning and optional structured prosthesis parameters. The retrospective cohort comprised 1,355 hips from 1,149 patients undergoing primary THA. Following rigid registration of postoperative CT to preoperative CT, the actual postoperative prostheses were transformed independently according to the pelvic and femoral registrations and represented as a dual-channel truncated signed distance field. The prosthesis autoencoder achieved a peak signal-to-noise ratio of 47.11 dB and a structural similarity index of 0.9964 on the validation set. Complete acetabular and femoral geometries were generated across seven major stem models representing 93.4% of the cohort. Repeated bone-conditioned sampling preserved component position, alignment, and the principal bone-prosthesis interfaces while allowing limited local geometric variation. To our knowledge, THA-Flow represents the first application of generative AI to three-dimensional surgical planning for THA.

摘要（中文）: 全髋关节置换术（THA）的术前规划通常被理解为针对患者骨骼解剖结构选择单一的假体构型与植入位置。然而在临床实践中，同一解剖结构可能对应多种临床可行方案，这使得规划本质上成为一个"一对多"问题，更适合用条件概率分布来表征。我们提出THA-Flow模型，这是一种条件流匹配模型，可直接从术前CT生成三维假体几何结构。独立的AutoencoderKL模型分别压缩术前骨骼解剖结构与假体几何结构，而三维UNet则学习从高斯噪声到假体潜在空间的校正流，该过程受空间骨骼条件约束及可选的结构化假体参数调控。回顾性队列包含1149例患者的1355个髋关节，均接受初次THA。将术后CT与术前CT进行刚性配准后，实际术后假体根据骨盆和股骨的配准结果独立变换，并表示为双通道截断符号距离场。在验证集上，假体自编码器的峰值信噪比达到47.11 dB，结构相似性指数为0.9964。模型生成了覆盖队列中93.4%病例的七种主要股骨柄模型的完整髋臼与股骨几何结构。重复的骨骼条件采样保持了组件位置、对位关系及主要骨-假体界面，同时允许有限的局部几何变异。据我们所知，THA-Flow代表了生成式人工智能在THA三维手术规划中的首次应用。

TLDR: THA-Flow is a conditional flow-matching model that generates 3D prosthesis geometry from preoperative CT scans for total hip arthroplasty, using separate autoencoders for bone anatomy and prosthesis geometry, and demonstrates high accuracy and clinical relevance.

CRESSim-Neo: A Batched GPU Simulation Engine for Surgical Robotics and Robot Learning

Abstract (English): We introduce CRESSim-Neo, a batched GPU simulation engine for surgical robotics and robot learning. CRESSim-Neo combines position-based simulation of rigid bodies, deformable tissues, fluids, and strands with batched rendering, surgery-specific sensing, and a GPU-resident data pipeline. The engine supports applications including tissue manipulation, fluid suction, suturing, cable-driven robots, and ultrasound image synthesis. Direct access to physics and rendering buffers enables GPU-resident robot learning and zero-copy PyTorch integration using DLPack. We demonstrate CRESSim-Neo across rigid-body, deformable-body, and fluid simulation tasks, including vision-based and surgical robot-learning scenarios. On an NVIDIA RTX 4090, the engine achieves up to 2.03 million environment steps per second for 8192 parallel CartPole environments, and scales to batched surgical scenarios involving tissue deformation, fluid interaction, and ultrasound sensing. Overall, CRESSim-Neo provides a unified and scalable platform for surgical simulation, synthetic data generation, and surgical robot learning.

摘要（中文）: 我们推出CRESSim-Neo，一种用于手术机器人与机器人学习的批处理GPU仿真引擎。CRESSim-Neo将刚体、可变形组织、流体及绳索的基于位置仿真，与批处理渲染、手术专用传感以及GPU驻留数据流水线相结合。该引擎支持组织操作、流体抽吸、缝合、线驱动机器人及超声图像合成等应用。通过直接访问物理与渲染缓冲区，可实现GPU驻留的机器人学习，并利用DLPack与PyTorch进行零拷贝集成。我们在刚体、可变形体及流体仿真任务中展示了CRESSim-Neo的性能，涵盖基于视觉与手术机器人学习场景。在NVIDIA RTX 4090上，该引擎对8192个并行CartPole环境达到每秒203万个环境步，并扩展至涉及组织变形、流体交互及超声传感的批处理手术场景。总之，CRESSim-Neo为手术仿真、合成数据生成及手术机器人学习提供了一个统一且可扩展的平台。

TLDR: CRESSim-Neo is a batched GPU simulation engine for surgical robotics that combines position-based simulation of rigid bodies, deformable tissues, fluids, and strands with surgery-specific sensing and a GPU-resident data pipeline, enabling high-speed parallel simulation (up to 2M steps/s on 8192 environments) and zero-copy PyTorch integration for robot learning.

Control-Oriented Learning for Dynamic Tracking and Stability Analysis of Soft Pneumatic Actuators

Abstract (English): Soft pneumatic actuators offer inherent compliance and safe interaction but remain difficult to model and control because of their highly nonlinear, distributed dynamics. We present a control-oriented data-driven modeling and control framework that decomposes actuator behavior into a nonlinear static equilibrium model and a linear residual dynamics model identified using Extended Dynamic Mode Decomposition with control (EDMDc). This representation enables feedforward compensation, task-space feedback control, and local closed-loop stability analysis through an augmented linear model. Experiments achieve approximately 1 mm root mean square error (RMSE) during low-speed (approximately 10 mm/s) trajectory tracking and below 10 mm RMSE at higher speeds (approximately 100 mm/s). The framework further achieves stable tracking of highly dynamic user-generated references with peak accelerations exceeding 25 m/s^2 while simultaneously performing real-time obstacle avoidance. Finally, the proposed stability analysis is experimentally validated by accurately predicting stable, marginal, and unstable operating regimes. These results demonstrate that structured, control-oriented learning provides an accurate and practical framework for soft actuator control.

摘要（中文）: 软体气动执行器具有固有的柔顺性和安全交互能力，但由于其高度非线性和分布式的动力学特性，建模与控制仍面临挑战。我们提出了一种面向控制的数据驱动建模与控制框架，该框架将执行器行为分解为非线性静态平衡模型和线性残差动力学模型，其中残差模型通过带控制的扩展动态模态分解（EDMDc）进行辨识。这种表示方法实现了前馈补偿、任务空间反馈控制，并通过增广线性模型进行局部闭环稳定性分析。实验结果表明：在低速（约10 mm/s）轨迹跟踪中，均方根误差（RMSE）约为1 mm；在高速（约100 mm/s）下，RMSE低于10 mm。该框架还能在实时避障的同时，稳定跟踪峰值加速度超过25 m/s²的高动态用户生成参考轨迹。最后，通过准确预测稳定、临界和不稳定工作区域，实验验证了所提出的稳定性分析方法。这些结果表明，结构化的、面向控制的学习方法为软体执行器控制提供了准确且实用的框架。

TLDR: This paper presents a control-oriented data-driven framework for soft pneumatic actuators, decomposing dynamics into nonlinear static and linear residual models using EDMDc, achieving precise trajectory tracking and validated stability analysis.

Large Language Model Few-Shot Prompting with Dilemma Training Outperforms Human Surrogates in Predicting Patient Preferences

Abstract (English): In serious illness, human surrogates often struggle to accurately predict patient preferences (68% accuracy), causing decision conflict. Personalized Patient Preference Predictor (P4) agents offer a potential solution, but prior prototypes treat values as static ratings, ignoring the contextual, situation-dependent nature of medical choices. Grounded in the 'logic of care', we present P4-DT (Dilemma Training), a P4 agent that constructs a patient decision policy by engaging users with varied medical dilemmas, eliciting individual preference reasoning through bi-directional training. In a study with 12 patient-surrogate dyads, P4-DT predicted patient treatment choices with 81.7% accuracy, significantly exceeding chance (OR = 5.61 [2.03, 15.51], p < .001) and outperforming both unassisted surrogates (55.0%; OR = 3.67 [1.59, 8.47], p = .002) and surrogates assisted by P4-DT (61.7%). Comparative prompt analyses showed that incorporating contextual scenario decisions and open-ended text improved accuracy by 15.0 percentage points over initial values ratings alone. We discuss implications for further testing and designing of context-aware AI agents that embody richer human experience to partner in complex decision-making.

Steer the Sampling, Not the Kernel Grid: Geometry-Guided Sampling Operator for Volumetric Segmentation

Abstract (English): Accurate 3D segmentation is central to quantitative lesion assessment and anatomy mapping for clinical planning and follow-up. Thin, elongated, and fine anatomical/pathological structures (e.g., vessels) are a particularly challenging case: a one-voxel boundary error can disconnect a branch and change clinically relevant topology. In encoder-decoder networks (e.g., U-Net), repeated downsampling and fixed-grid convolution blur or alias fine structures and weaken orientation cues, so early mistakes propagate across scales. We propose a geometry-guided local operator that steers where features are sampled, rather than deforming convolutional kernels, under a single formulation for both feature refinement (stride 1) and resolution reduction (stride > 1). At each voxel, it predicts a local orientation and bounded step sizes, samples symmetrically along these directions, and transforms paired samples into compact geometric and boundary cues with lightweight mixing; a cross-scale consensus aligns encoder and decoder features at skip connections to reduce geometric mismatch. Replacing all stride 1 and stride 2 operators in a 3D U-Net yields consistent improvements on BraTS, MSD Hepatic Vessel, and TDSC-ABUS, with notably better boundary metrics (e.g., BraTS Dice 86.1 to 88.9, HD95 7.1 to 6.2; TDSC-ABUS HD95 39.1 to 27.8) while reducing parameters from 2.3M to 0.8M. We further demonstrate that the operator can be integrated into other backbones (e.g., nnU-Net, Swin-UNETR, and MedNeXt) without changing their macro-architectures while providing consistent performance gains.

摘要（中文）: 准确的3D分割是临床规划与随访中定量病灶评估和解剖结构映射的核心。薄而细长的精细解剖/病理结构（如血管）尤为具有挑战性：一个体素的边界误差即可导致分支断裂，并改变临床相关的拓扑结构。在编码器-解码器网络（如U-Net）中，重复下采样和固定网格卷积会模糊或混叠精细结构、削弱方向线索，导致早期误差跨尺度传播。我们提出了一种几何引导的局部算子，该算子在不扭曲卷积核的情况下，通过统一公式控制特征采样位置（既可用于步长为1的特征细化，也可用于步长大于1的分辨率降低）。在每个体素处，该算子预测局部方向和有界步长，沿这些方向对称采样，并通过轻量混合将配对样本转换为紧凑的几何与边界线索；跨尺度一致性机制在跳跃连接处对齐编码器与解码器特征，以减少几何失配。将3D U-Net中所有步长为1和步长为2的算子替换为该算子后，在BraTS、MSD肝血管和TDSC-ABUS数据集上均取得一致改进，边界指标显著提升（例如BraTS Dice从86.1升至88.9，HD95从7.1降至6.2；TDSC-ABUS HD95从39.1降至27.8），同时参数量从230万降至80万。我们进一步证明，该算子可集成到其他骨干网络（如nnU-Net、Swin-UNETR和MedNeXt）中，无需改变其宏观架构即可带来持续的性能增益。

Label-Free Foundational Model Selection for Medical Image Classification under Distribution Shift via Pseudo Label Discrepancy

Abstract (English): Foundation models are increasingly deployed for medical image analysis. However, under the inter-institutional distribution shift typical of deployment, their performance varies widely and cannot be known without target-domain labels, which are rarely available. This leaves a practical question unresolved: given several candidate foundational models and labeled-data from a source domain, which one to deploy in an unlabeled target domain? We propose a label-free selection criterion built on SUDO, a framework for evaluating clinical AI systems without ground-truth annotations. SUDO partitions the unlabeled target data by predicted probability and, for each region, measures a pseudo-label discrepancy reflecting class contamination; aggregated across regions, this yields a score (AURCC) requiring neither target annotation nor fine-tuning. We show that AURCC can be used to rank a variety of vision-language models (BioMedCLIP, CXR-CLIP, CheXzero, MedCLIP, MedImageInsight, CLIP) on chest X-ray classification across three inter-hospital shift scenarios, under zero-shot and MLP-probe regimes. The AURCC ranking recovers the ground-truth ranking with Spearman rho up to 0.943 (p<0.05). Against the natural baseline of ranking by held-out source accuracy, AURCC is competitive when the labeled source is large and yields a more accurate ranking once it is small; the regime of interest in resource-constrained settings.

摘要（中文）: 摘要： 基础模型越来越多地应用于医学图像分析。然而，在部署中典型的机构间分布偏移下，这些模型的性能差异很大，且由于目标域标签极少可用而无法预知。这留下了一个实际未解的问题：给定多个候选基础模型及来自源域的有标签数据，应选择哪一个部署至无标签的目标域？我们提出了一种基于SUDO的无标签选择标准——SUDO是一种无需真实标注即可评估临床AI系统的框架。SUDO根据预测概率对无标签目标数据进行分区，并为每个区域测量反映类别污染的伪标签差异；跨区域汇总后，得到一个无需目标域标注或微调的评分（AURCC）。我们证明，在零样本和MLP探针设置下，AURCC可用于对多种视觉-语言模型（BioMedCLIP、CXR-CLIP、CheXzero、MedCLIP、MedImageInsight、CLIP）在三种医院间偏移场景下的胸部X光分类进行排序。AURCC排序恢复真实排序的斯皮尔曼相关系数高达0.943（p<0.05）。与基于保留源域准确率排序的自然基线相比，当有标签源域数据量大时AURCC具有竞争力，而当数据量小时能给出更准确的排序——这正是资源受限场景下所关注的设置。

TLDR: The paper proposes a label-free method (AURCC) to rank foundation models for medical image classification under distribution shift, using pseudo-label discrepancy on unlabeled target data, and validates it on chest X-ray tasks.
