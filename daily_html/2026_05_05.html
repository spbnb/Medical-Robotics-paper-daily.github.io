Shared Autonomy Assisted by Impedance-Driven Anisotropic Guidance Field

Abstract (English): Shared autonomy (SA) enables robots to infer human intent and assist in its achievement. While most research focuses on improving intent inference, it overlooks whether humans can understand the robot's intent in return. Without such mutual understanding, collaboration becomes less effective, degrading user experience and task performance. To address this gap, previous studies have explicitly conveyed the robot intent through additional interfaces, which remain unintuitive and limited in expressiveness. Inspired by impedance control, we propose Impedance-Driven Anisotropic Guidance Field Enhanced Shared Autonomy (IAGF-SA), a novel paradigm that extends SA with an embodied, physically-grounded communication channel. This channel adaptively modulates the robot's dynamic response to human input, enabling intuitive, continuous, physically-grounded robot intent communication while naturally guiding human actions. User studies across three scenarios and two teleoperation interfaces indicate that IAGF-SA improves task performance, human-robot agreement, and subjective experience, thus demonstrating its effectiveness in enhancing human-robot communication and collaboration.

摘要（中文）: 共享自主性（SA）使机器人能够推断人类意图并协助实现目标。现有研究大多聚焦于提升意图推断能力，却忽视了人类能否同步理解机器人意图的问题。缺乏这种双向理解会削弱协作效率，降低用户体验与任务执行效果。针对这一局限，先前研究主要通过附加接口显式传递机器人意图，但此类方式仍不够直观且表达能力有限。受阻抗控制启发，我们提出一种阻抗驱动的各向异性导引场增强型共享自主性（IAGF-SA）新范式。该范式通过具身化、物理基础的通信通道扩展了传统SA框架，能自适应调节机器人对人类输入的动态响应，在自然引导人类操作的同时，实现直观、连续、基于物理机制的机器人意图传递。在三种场景与两种遥操作接口下的用户研究表明，IAGF-SA能有效提升任务执行效率、人机协同一致性及主观体验，从而验证了其增强人机通信与协作能力的有效性。

TLDR: This paper proposes IAGF-SA, a shared autonomy method that uses impedance control to create a physical guidance field, allowing the robot to intuitively convey its intent back to the human operator while assisting them, thereby improving mutual understanding and collaboration in teleoperation tasks.

Semantic Risk-Aware Heuristic Planning for Robotic Navigation in Dynamic Environments: An LLM-Inspired Approach

Abstract (English): The integration of Large Language Model (LLM) reasoning principles into classical robot path planning represents a rapidly emerging research direction. In this paper, we propose a Semantic Risk-Aware Heuristic (SRAH) planner that encodes LLM-inspired cost functions penalising geometrically cluttered or high-risk zones into an A$^*$ search framework, augmented with closed-loop replanning upon dynamic obstacle detection. We evaluate SRAH against two established baselines Breadth-First Search (BFS) with replanning and a Greedy heuristic without replanning across 200 randomised trials in a $15{\times}15$ grid-world with 20\% static obstacle density and stochastic dynamic obstacles. SRAH achieves a task success rate of 62.0\%, outperforming BFS (56.5\%) by 9.7\% relative improvement and Greedy (4.0\%) by a large margin. We further analyse the trade-off between planning overhead, path efficiency, and failure-recovery count, and demonstrate via an obstacle-density ablation that semantic cost shaping consistently improves navigation across environments of varying difficulty. Our results suggest that even lightweight, LLM-inspired heuristics provide measurable safety and robustness gains for autonomous robot navigation.

摘要（中文）: 将大语言模型（LLM）的推理机制融入经典机器人路径规划，已成为一个快速兴起的研究方向。本文提出了一种语义风险感知启发式（SRAH）规划器，该规划器将受LLM启发的成本函数编码至A*搜索框架中，对几何结构杂乱或高风险区域施加惩罚，并引入动态障碍物检测后的闭环重规划机制。我们在一个15×15网格世界（静态障碍物密度20%且存在随机动态障碍）中进行了200次随机试验，将SRAH与两种现有基线方法——带重规划的广度优先搜索（BFS）以及不带重规划的贪心启发式算法——进行对比评估。SRAH的任务成功率达到62.0%，相对于BFS（56.5%）实现了9.7%的相对提升，并显著优于贪心算法（4.0%）。我们进一步分析了规划开销、路径效率与故障恢复次数之间的权衡关系，并通过障碍物密度消融实验证明：语义成本塑形能持续提升算法在不同难度环境中的导航性能。实验结果表明，即使是轻量级的、受LLM启发的启发式方法，也能为自主机器人导航带来可量化的安全性与鲁棒性提升。

It demonstrates improved success rates over classical baselines in a grid-world with dynamic obstacles, suggesting lightweight LLM-inspired heuristics can enhance safety and robustness.
