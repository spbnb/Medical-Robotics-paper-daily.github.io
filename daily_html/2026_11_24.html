SurgeCount: A Protocol-Guided Composition Harness for Bimanual Surgical Sponge Counting

Abstract (English): We present SurgeCount, a system for auditable robotic surgical sponge counting that composes learned skills under a written procedural protocol. Retained sponges cause preventable harm, and surgical teams count every sponge before closure to reduce that risk. The robot repeatedly isolates one deformable sponge from a tangled pile and places it in the next numbered counting-tray slot, requiring handling of occluded grasp threads while preserving step order. We train one subtask-conditioned policy on eleven short skills and learn when each skill ends. At each learned boundary, a stateful vision-language model selects the next skill from an operator-authored specification of step order, visual verification, and recovery. This creates temporal commitment to each subtask and enables failures to be recovered by recomposing existing skills rather than using a dedicated recovery policy. We evaluate five methods across 100 real-robot trials using 20 matched layouts. SurgeCount fills 47 of 60 ordered slots and completes 12 of 20 procedures, compared with 27 of 60 and 3 of 20 for the strongest of four end-to-end policies trained on the same data. Replacing the detailed protocol prompt with a minimal prompt reduces performance to 29 of 60 slots and 5 of 20 completed procedures. These results show that long-horizon performance depends not only on visuomotor capacity, but also on how learned skills are committed, verified, and composed. This tabletop proof of concept motivates further evaluation of protocol-guided robotic sponge counting in clinically representative settings.

MedUIGym: Evaluating and Training GUI Agents for Clinical Workflows

Abstract (English): Automating clinical workflows requires vision-language agents that can navigate dense graphical user interfaces (GUIs) in electronic health records (EHRs) and execute precise actions, yet many medical agents still rely on text or structured tool calls. In this work, we introduce MedUIGym, a scalable interactive environment comprising 15,739 tasks from 17 benchmark sources across six clinical task families for training and evaluating clinical GUI agents. In MedUIGym, agents perceive EHR states from screenshots and execute mouse-and-keyboard actions, as well as a paired native-tool interface over identical clinical data. The environment verifies both final answers and required clinical actions, and supports parallel trajectory collection for training. Benchmarking twelve vision-language models shows that current agents struggle across diverse clinical workflows, and simply increasing reasoning effort fails to yield consistent gains. Furthermore, MedUIGym supports offline learning from GUI trajectories and online reinforcement learning from task rewards. After training, both Qwen3.5 models perform better on in-domain clinical tasks and generalize to tasks from external sources. By unifying tasks from existing benchmarks, dual-interface evaluation, and GUI trajectory collection, MedUIGym provides a testbed for studying how vision-language agents complete clinical tasks through the EHR's graphical interface. Our project page is available at https://github.com/yitong-qiao/MedUIGym.

ColonoWorld: Camera-Controlled Full-Procedure Colonoscopy Video Generation

Abstract (English): Navigation systems in colonoscopy are advancing from guiding the endoscopist to steering the scope autonomously. Since an incorrect motion can injure the patient, developing and evaluating these systems calls for a world model of colonoscopy. Such a model must follow a commanded scope motion, keep the colon anatomy consistent, and show lesions and procedural events. Yet camera-controlled generators trained on general scenes lose the colon's structure under motion, and endoscopic generators offer no camera control. We present ColonoWorld, a colonoscopy world model conditioned on a camera trajectory and a coarse 3D tube of the colon, trained on in vivo videos. From a whole-colon tube, it generates a full cecum-to-rectum procedure, even for unseen colon shapes. It also places polyps, instruments, and irrigation at specified locations and times. ColonoWorld outperforms state-of-the-art video generators, reducing camera trajectory error by 32% and FVD by 29%. Our code and annotations will be released at https://aidaslab.github.io/ColonoWorld.

Flagging Batch Shift in Flow Cytometry using Jeffreys Divergence between Gaussian Mixtures

Abstract (English): Machine learning models for clinical flow cytometry are usually evaluated on data drawn from their training distribution. In routine practice, however, acquisition conditions and reagents may change, potentially making predictions unreliable without warning. We propose a model agnostic criterion for prediction reliability based on a notion of distance from the training distribution: each patient sample is represented by a Gaussian mixture model, and its Jeffreys divergence to a reference fitted on the training data is estimated using Monte Carlo integration. We illustrate our approach on real-life shifted cohorts of acute myeloid leukaemia patients, using a classifier trained to predict NPM1 mutation status. First, we show graphically and statistically that pairwise divergences capture acquisition cohort structure. We then calibrate a rejection threshold on a subset of shifted cohort samples and evaluate it on held-out subjects. Across 20 runs, the misclassification rate of rejected samples was 2.5 times larger than for retained samples. These results suggest that input divergence can help identify unreliable predictions, providing a strong candidate component for abstention frameworks, along with quality control measures and classifier confidence.

摘要（中文）: 用于临床流式细胞术的机器学习模型通常基于其训练分布中的数据进行评估。然而，在常规实践中，采集条件和试剂可能发生变化，从而可能使预测在无预警的情况下变得不可靠。我们提出了一种与模型无关的预测可靠性判据，该判据基于与训练分布之间距离的概念：每个患者样本用高斯混合模型表示，并通过蒙特卡洛积分估计其与在训练数据上拟合的参考分布之间的杰弗里斯散度。我们在真实世界中发生偏移的急性髓系白血病患者队列上展示了该方法，使用一个训练用于预测NPM1突变状态的分类器。首先，我们从图形和统计上证明成对散度能够捕捉采集队列的结构。然后，我们在偏移队列样本的子集上校准拒绝阈值，并在留出受试者上进行评估。在20次运行中，被拒绝样本的误分类率是保留样本的2.5倍。这些结果表明，输入散度有助于识别不可靠的预测，为弃权框架连同质量控制措施和分类器置信度提供了一个强有力的候选组件。

Laya-IsoToken: Day-One Clinical Codebook Switching via Closed-Form Operator Compilation

Abstract (English): Healthcare systems periodically revise diagnostic and procedural classifications (ICD-9 to ICD-10/11), update hospital reimbursement groupers (MS-DRG), and deploy clinical coding across languages. Supervised coding networks bind clinical text encoders to fixed per-code classifier weights, forcing hospitals to re-annotate tens of thousands of records before switching codebooks, whereas standalone large language models incur high latency and hallucinate invalid codes. Here we show that day-one clinical codebook switching becomes feasible without target-labeled notes when local clinical evidence reading is decoupled from a strictly frozen multilingual concept space. Laya-IsoToken trains a clinical span reader once on source records with zero per-code classifier parameters, separating text evidence from historical disease prevalence. Given only public target code descriptions, closed-form matrix compilation aligns distributional shifts, compensates for paraphrased definitions, and deduplicates sibling codes in under three seconds, while a pre-deployment geometric residual routes unfamiliar concepts to offline language model verification. Across 32,804 MIMIC-IV ICD-9 discharges, Laya-IsoToken matches published supervised baselines (62.6% Micro-F1) while reducing active calibration error by 32%. Switched with zero target training notes to 13,783 unseen-patient ICD-10 discharges, MS-DRG groups, and multilingual cohorts, it outperforms biomedical dense retrievers, semantic crosswalks (+5.4 F1 points on ICD-10, paired $d_z = 0.49$, $p < 0.001$), and official US CMS equivalence mappings (+3.5 points, $p < 0.001$) at 9.4 ms per admission. Pairing this reader with an offline 31B verifier across 1,000 full discharge summaries lifts zero-training ICD-10 coding to 40.6% Micro-F1 (57.0% category F1, $d_z = 0.66$, $p < 0.001$) with 0.0% invalid code hallucinations.

WaDiS: Boundary-Preserving Diffusion Segmentation via Multi-Scale Wavelet Conditioning and Frequency-Aware Gating

Abstract (English): Diffusion models have shown strong potential in medical image segmentation due to their generative capabilities and robustness. However, existing methods often struggle to preserve fine anatomical boundaries and to retain multi-scale structural information due to the loss of spatial and frequency details during feature encoding, particularly in complex clinical imaging conditions. This work presents WaDiS, a wavelet-powered conditional latent diffusion framework for medical image segmentation that addresses these limitations through multi-scale wavelet conditioning and frequency-aware information filtering for boundary preservation. The model adopts a multi-scale wavelet conditioning encoder to retain rich structural features, leveraging its lossless nature to better preserve semantic priors critical to the diffusion process. In addition, a Frequency-Aware Gating Block (FAGB) is introduced into the skip connections of the diffusion U-Net, enabling adaptive spectral filtering and residual fusion to better emphasize boundary structures while reducing irrelevant content. To improve generalization across clinical domains, we adopt a structure-preserving color augmentation strategy during training. Specifically, global color statistics are transferred between samples in the LAB color space, allowing the model to decouple appearance from structure and focus more on anatomical shape rather than color bias. Experiments on five benchmark datasets demonstrate that WaDiS consistently outperforms existing diffusion-based and conventional segmentation methods, with notable improvements in boundary accuracy, robustness to noise, and generalization under color variation. The code for this work will be made publicly available upon acceptance.

摘要（中文）: 扩散模型凭借其生成能力和鲁棒性，在医学图像分割中展现出强大潜力。然而，现有方法在特征编码过程中常因空间和频率细节的丢失，难以保留精细的解剖边界及多尺度结构信息，尤其是在复杂的临床成像条件下。本文提出WaDiS，一种基于小波的条件下潜在扩散医学图像分割框架，通过多尺度小波条件化与频率感知信息过滤来解决上述局限性，实现边界保留。该模型采用多尺度小波条件编码器来保留丰富的结构特征，利用其无损特性更好地保持对扩散过程至关重要的语义先验。此外，在扩散U-Net的跳跃连接中引入频率感知门控块（FAGB），实现自适应频谱滤波与残差融合，从而在抑制无关内容的同时突出边界结构。为提升跨临床领域的泛化能力，我们在训练期间采用结构保持的颜色增强策略。具体而言，在LAB颜色空间中将全局颜色统计信息在样本间迁移，使模型能够将外观与结构解耦，更多关注解剖形状而非颜色偏差。在五个基准数据集上的实验表明，WaDiS始终优于现有的基于扩散的方法和传统分割方法，在边界准确性、噪声鲁棒性及颜色变化下的泛化能力方面均有显著提升。本工作代码将在录用后公开发布。
