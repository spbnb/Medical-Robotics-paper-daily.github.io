On Energy-Based Modelling for Fluidic Excitable Neurons

Abstract (English): Motivated by the potential of pneumatic excitable devices in soft robotics, this paper develops an energy-based, domain-agnostic equivalent of the Hodgkin-Huxley modelling framework using bond graph theory. The proposed model is used to guide the design of a pneumatic excitable neuron. The potential of this approach is illustrated through the design of a pneumatic half-center oscillator, an essential building block for rhythmic neuromorphic behaviours.

摘要（中文）: 受气动可兴奋装置在软体机器人领域应用潜力的启发，本文基于键合图理论，发展了一种能量基、领域无关的Hodgkin-Huxley建模框架等效形式。所提出的模型用于指导气动可兴奋神经元的设计。通过设计一个气动半中心振荡器——节律性神经形态行为的关键构建模块——展示了该方法的潜力。

TLDR: The paper proposes an energy-based, domain-agnostic Hodgkin-Huxley-style modeling framework using bond graph theory, and demonstrates its use in designing a pneumatic excitable neuron and a pneumatic half-center oscillator for soft robotic rhythmic behaviors.

Optical fiber shape sensing using picosecond spatiotemporal single-photon reflectometry

Abstract (English): Inferring the three-dimensional configuration of a flexible optical fiber from a single accessible end remains a fundamental challenge in photonic sensing. Existing approaches typically require specialised multicore fibers, coherent interferometry, or distributed spectral encoding. Here, we demonstrate a fundamentally different sensing paradigm in which fiber geometry is reconstructed from the spatiotemporal dynamics of returning single photons. Using a laser-engineered multimode fiber interrogated by a time-resolved 32x32 single-photon avalanche diode array, we show that fiber deformation imprints unique signatures onto the distribution and arrival time of back-reflected photons. We map these high-dimensional photon-return states onto a low-dimensional geometric manifold, enabling continuous bend-angle estimation and reconstruction of previously unseen two- and three-dimensional shapes from measurements acquired entirely at the proximal fiber end. Measurements show a median pixel-wise signal-to-noise ratio of approximately 26 dB and below 1% repeatability variation. Our results establish multimode single-photon dynamics as an information-rich channel for distributed geometric sensing, introducing a new framework for single-ended fiber shape reconstruction and minimally invasive photonic navigation.

摘要（中文）: 从单一可接触端推断柔性光纤的三维构型，一直是光子传感领域的基本挑战。现有方法通常需要特种多芯光纤、相干干涉测量或分布式光谱编码。在此，我们展示了一种根本不同的传感范式，即从返回单光子的时空动力学中重建光纤几何形状。利用激光工程化的多模光纤，并由时间分辨的32×32单光子雪崩二极管阵列进行 interrogated，我们表明光纤形变会在背向反射光子的分布和到达时间上印下独特特征。我们将这些高维光子返回态映射到低维几何流形上，从而能够完全基于在光纤近端获取的测量数据，实现连续弯曲角估计以及先前未见过的二维和三维形状重建。测量结果显示，中位像素信噪比约为26 dB，重复性变异低于1%。我们的结果确立了多模单光子动力学作为分布式几何传感的信息丰富信道，为单端光纤形状重建和微创光子导航引入了一种新框架。

Authors: Harikumar K Chandrasekharan, Saba N. Khan, Muhammad Afnan Ansari, Robert R. Thomson

A risk-aware individualized blood pressure target to avoid hypotension in surgery

Abstract (English): We propose an online method for choosing both a blood pressure target and a norepinephrine dose for an individual surgical patient. From a short record of mean arterial pressure (MAP) and norepinephrine delivery, the method fits a reduced pharmacokinetic/pharmacodynamic model and uses a local Gaussian approximation to propagate uncertainty to future MAP readings. It searches for the lowest target for which a bolus and constant infusion keep the predicted probability of at least one MAP reading below 65 mmHg after the rise phase within a prescribed limit. If no such target is found, the decision returns to the clinician. We assess calibration of the predicted minimum MAP on retrospective surgical records under the doses actually administered. In a cohort of 4,096 virtual patients, the method recommended a dose for 74 % of patients at a nominal risk of 5 %. Among the patients receiving a recommendation, 6.9 % had a reading below 65 mmHg. A fixed target of 70 mmHg yielded a rate of 25 % when applied to the full virtual cohort.

摘要（中文）: 我们提出了一种在线方法，用于为个体手术患者选择血压目标及去甲肾上腺素剂量。该方法根据平均动脉压（MAP）和去甲肾上腺素给药的短期记录，拟合一个简化的药代动力学/药效学模型，并利用局部高斯近似将不确定性传播至未来的MAP读数。它搜索最低的血压目标，使得在该目标下，一次推注加持续输注能够将上升阶段后至少一次MAP读数低于65 mmHg的预测概率控制在规定限值以内。若未找到此类目标，则决策权交还临床医生。我们利用实际给药剂量下的回顾性手术记录，评估了预测最低MAP的校准情况。在包含4，096例虚拟患者的队列中，该方法在名义风险为5%的情况下为74%的患者推荐了剂量。在获得推荐的患者中，6.9%出现低于65 mmHg的读数。而将固定目标设为70 mmHg时，应用于整个虚拟队列的低于65 mmHg发生率为25%。

TLDR: Presents an online decision-support method that personalizes mean arterial pressure targets and norepinephrine dosing during surgery, using a pharmacokinetic/pharmacodynamic model and uncertainty propagation to limit predicted hypotension risk; simulated evaluation shows reduced hypotensive episodes versus a fixed target.

Authors: Simon Bur{é}, Jona Joachim, Florent Di Meglio

Parameter-Efficient Conciseness Adaptation: Differentiable Length Control for Concise Clinical Reasoning in Smaller Language Models

Abstract (English): Smaller language models (SLMs) make it feasible to deploy clinical natural language processing where large proprietary models are too costly, and parameter-efficient fine-tuning allows them to be specialised on commodity hardware. One obstacle persists after training: on medical tasks these models answer at far greater length than the clinical question requires. In clinical decision support and point-of-care use, where time is short, such verbosity is hard to act on even when the underlying reasoning is sound. Existing length-control methods depend on preference optimisation, reward models, or proprietary teachers that undercut the accessibility making SLMs attractive. We introduce Parameter-Efficient Conciseness Adaptation (PECA), which adds a single differentiable conciseness term to the QLoRA supervised fine-tuning objective. Because the token count is discrete and non-differentiable, PECA instead raises the end-of-sequence probability once generation exceeds a token budget, a smooth signal optimised during ordinary training, with no preference data, no auxiliary model, and a single commodity GPU. On a 1.7-billion-parameter model fine-tuned on the medical-o1-reasoning-SFT corpus, PECA reduces mean response length by 72.8% while improving multiple-choice diagnostic accuracy from 40.8% to 44.0%. A sweep over the conciseness strength traces an interpretable accuracy-to-length frontier for balancing brevity and diagnostic completeness.

摘要（中文）: 较小语言模型（SLMs）使得在大型专有模型成本过高的场景中部署临床自然语言处理成为可能，而参数高效微调则允许在商用硬件上对模型进行专门化。训练之后仍有一个障碍：在这些医学任务上，模型回答的篇幅远超临床问题所需。在临床决策支持和护理点应用中，时间紧迫，即使底层推理是正确的，这种冗长回答也难以付诸实践。现有的长度控制方法依赖于偏好优化、奖励模型或专有教师模型，这些都会削弱SLMs吸引人的可及性。我们提出了参数高效简洁性适配（PECA），该方法在QLoRA监督微调目标中添加了一个单一的可微简洁性损失项。由于token计数是离散且不可微的，PECA改为在生成超过token预算时提高结束序列概率，这是一个在普通训练期间优化的平滑信号，无需偏好数据、无需辅助模型、只需一块商用GPU。在一个基于medical-o1-reasoning-SFT语料库微调的17亿参数模型上，PECA将平均回答长度减少了72.8%，同时将多项选择诊断准确率从40.8%提升至44.0%。对简洁性强度的扫描描绘出一条可解释的准确率-长度边界曲线，用于平衡简洁性与诊断完整性。

TLDR: PECA adds a single differentiable conciseness term to the QLoRA supervised fine-tuning objective, raising the end-of-sequence probability when generation exceeds a token budget to discourage verbose clinical reasoning.
