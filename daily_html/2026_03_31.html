Probe-to-Grasp Manipulation Using Self-Sensing Pneumatic Variable-Stiffness Joints

Abstract (English): Grasping deformable objects with varying stiffness remains a significant challenge in robotics. Estimating the local stiffness of a target object is important for determining an optimal grasp pose that enables stable pickup without damaging the object. This paper presents a probe-to-grasp manipulation framework for estimating the relative stiffness of objects using a passive soft-rigid two-finger hybrid gripper equipped with self-sensing pneumatic variable-stiffness joints. Each finger of the gripper consists of two rigid links connected by a soft pneumatic ring placed at the joint, enabling both compliant interaction and controllable joint stiffness via internal pressurization. By measuring the pressure inside the pneumatic ring, we can estimate the interaction force during contact. Building on this, we propose a practical probing strategy to infer relative object stiffness by correlating the estimated normal force with known gripper closing displacement. We validate the self-sensing model through stiffness characterization experiments across bending angles and pressure ranges, and demonstrate stiffness-aware probing-and-grasping in real-life applications: selecting grasp locations on fruits with spatially varying stiffness. The proposed system offers a minimal, low-cost sensing approach for stiffness-aware soft manipulation while retaining probing and grasping capability.

摘要（中文）: 抓取具有不同刚度的可变形物体仍然是机器人领域的一个重要挑战。估计目标物体的局部刚度对于确定最佳抓取位姿至关重要，这样可以在稳定拾取物体的同时避免损坏物体。本文提出了一种基于探针到抓取的操作框架，使用配备自感测气压可变刚度关节的被动软硬混合两指夹持器来估计物体的相对刚度。夹持器的每个手指由两个刚性连杆组成，通过放置在关节处的气软环连接，通过内部加压实现顺应性交互和可控的关节刚度。通过测量气软环内部的压力，我们可以估计接触时的相互作用力。在此基础上，我们提出了一种实用的探测策略，通过将估计的法向力与已知的夹持器闭合位移相关联来推断物体的相对刚度。我们通过在弯曲角度和压力范围内进行的刚度表征实验验证了自感测模型，并展示了在现实应用中的刚度感知探测与抓取：针对具有空间刚度变化的水果选择抓取位置。该系统提供了一种最小化、低成本的刚度感知软体操作传感方法，同时保留了探测和抓取能力。

PoseDreamer: Scalable and Photorealistic Human Data Generation Pipeline with Diffusion Models

Abstract (English): Acquiring labeled datasets for 3D human mesh estimation is challenging due to depth ambiguities and the inherent difficulty of annotating 3D geometry from monocular images. Existing datasets are either real, with manually annotated 3D geometry and limited scale, or synthetic, rendered from 3D engines that provide precise labels but suffer from limited photorealism, low diversity, and high production costs. In this work, we explore a third path: generated data. We introduce PoseDreamer, a novel pipeline that leverages diffusion models to generate large-scale synthetic datasets with 3D mesh annotations. Our approach combines controllable image generation with Direct Preference Optimization for control alignment, curriculum-based hard sample mining, and multi-stage quality filtering. Together, these components naturally maintain correspondence between 3D labels and generated images, while prioritizing challenging samples to maximize dataset utility. Using PoseDreamer, we generate more than 500,000 high-quality synthetic samples, achieving a 76% improvement in image-quality metrics compared to rendering-based datasets. Models trained on PoseDreamer achieve performance comparable to or superior to those trained on real-world and traditional synthetic datasets. In addition, combining PoseDreamer with synthetic datasets results in better performance than combining real-world and synthetic datasets, demonstrating the complementary nature of our dataset. We will release the full dataset and generation code.

摘要（中文）: 获取用于3D人体网格估计的标注数据集具有挑战性，原因在于深度模糊以及从单目图像标注3D几何的固有困难。现有数据集要么是真实的，包含手动标注的3D几何但规模有限，要么是合成的，由3D引擎渲染提供精确标签，但存在照片真实感不足、多样性有限以及生产成本高的问题。在本工作中，我们探索了第三条路径：生成式数据。我们提出了PoseDreamer，这是一种利用扩散模型生成大规模带3D网格标注的合成数据集的新颖管道。我们的方法结合了可控图像生成与直接偏好优化的控制对齐、基于课程的难例挖掘以及多阶段质量过滤。这些组件自然地维持了3D标签与生成图像之间的对应关系，同时优先考虑挑战性样本以最大化数据集效用。使用PoseDreamer，我们生成了超过50万个高质量合成样本，在图像质量指标上相比基于渲染的数据集实现了76%的提升。在PoseDreamer上训练的模型性能与在真实世界和传统合成数据集上训练的模型相当或更优。此外，将PoseDreamer与合成数据集结合使用比将真实世界与合成数据集结合使用效果更好，证明了我们数据集的互补性。我们将发布完整的数据集和生成代码。

TLDR: PoseDreamer introduces a diffusion‑model based pipeline that generates massive photorealistic human images paired with precise 3D mesh annotations, outperforming conventional rendering datasets and enabling models trained on it to reach state‑of‑the‑art accuracy.
