MedGraph: Maintaining Patient State with Graph Memory for Long-Term Medical Dialogue

Abstract (English): LLM-based medical assistants that follow a patient across visits need a memory that keeps the patient's clinical state correct as it changes. We examine the errors of five memory systems on longitudinal medical dialogue and find three recurring causes: the retrieved evidence gives an outdated state, includes another person's health facts, or lacks a safety fact the answer depends on. We present MedGraph, a graph memory built for these three cases. Construction extracts dated facts from each session, attributes each fact to the person it describes, and links medication, diagnosis, and measurement records into chains that keep earlier states. Retrieval ranks the patient's chains as units, reads each chain at the time the question asks about, and adds the original dialogue text; a patient profile lists every recorded safety fact with every question. On MedMemoryBench, MedGraph has the highest overall accuracy with both a GPT and a Qwen backbone, 10.5 and 10.4 points above the strongest of five baselines, with efficient memory construction and answering in the GPT setting.

摘要（中文）: 基于大语言模型的医疗助手在跨就诊随访患者时，需要一种能够随患者临床状态变化而保持其正确性的记忆系统。我们考察了五种记忆系统在纵向医疗对话中的错误，发现了三个反复出现的成因：检索到的证据呈现的是过时状态、包含他人的健康事实、或缺少答案所依赖的安全事实。我们提出MedGraph，一种针对这三种情况构建的图记忆。其构建过程从每次就诊中提取带日期的事实，将每个事实归属于其描述的对象，并将药物、诊断和测量记录链接成链，以保留早期状态。检索时将患者的链作为整体排序，在问题所询问的时间点读取每条链，并补充原始对话文本；患者档案则列出每条记录的安全事实及每个问题。在MedMemoryBench上，MedGraph在GPT和Qwen两种主干模型下均取得最高总体准确率，分别比五个基线中最强者高出10.5和10.4个百分点，且在GPT设置下具有高效的记忆构建与回答能力。

TLDR: MedGraph is a graph-based memory system for LLM medical assistants that tracks patient state across visits by extracting dated, person-attributed facts into chains of medications, diagnoses, and measurements. It outperforms five baselines on a longitudinal medical dialogue benchmark by 10+ points with both GPT and Qwen backbones.

简要说明: MedGraph 是一种面向LLM医疗助手的图记忆系统，通过提取带日期、归属明确的事实，并将药物、诊断和测量记录链接成链，来跨就诊维护患者状态。在纵向医疗对话基准上，它使用GPT和Qwen两种骨干模型均比五个基线高出10分以上。
