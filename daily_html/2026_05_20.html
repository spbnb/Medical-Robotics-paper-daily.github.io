Neural Operators for Design-Space Surrogate Modeling of Tendon-Actuated Continuum Robots

Abstract (English): Continuum robots enable dexterous manipulation in constrained environments, but require accurate and efficient models for real-time manipulation and control. Traditional physics-based models can be computationally expensive and may suffer from inaccuracies due to unmodeled effects, while current learning-based methods often generalize poorly beyond the specific robot on which they are trained. We present a formulation of surrogate modeling for tendon-driven continuum robots as an operator learning problem that maps robot design parameters and tendon actuation inputs to resulting configurations. This formulation enables a single trained model to generalize across a large class of robot designs. We develop four novel neural operator architectures--two based on Deep Operator Networks (DeepONets) and two based on Fourier Neural Operators (FNOs)--and train them on simulation data to predict robot configurations. All architectures achieve good accuracy while allowing for fast and accurate generalization across designs. Our results demonstrate that operator learning provides an effective and generalizable surrogate for continuum robot mechanics in the design space, enabling fast modeling for control, planning, and design optimization in surgical and industrial applications.

摘要（中文）: 连续体机器人能够在受限环境中实现灵巧操作，但需要精确高效的模型以支持实时操控。传统的基于物理的模型计算成本高昂，且可能因未建模效应导致精度不足，而当前基于学习的方法在训练对象之外的机器人上往往泛化能力较差。本文将肌腱驱动连续体机器人的代理建模问题构建为算子学习问题，其目标是将机器人设计参数与肌腱驱动输入映射至最终构型。该框架使得单个训练模型能够泛化至广泛类别的机器人设计。我们开发了四种新型神经算子架构——两种基于深度算子网络，两种基于傅里叶神经算子——并利用仿真数据训练它们以预测机器人构型。所有架构在实现高精度的同时，能够快速准确地在不同设计间泛化。结果表明，算子学习为连续体机器人在设计空间中的力学建模提供了高效且可泛化的代理方案，为手术和工业应用中的控制、路径规划与设计优化实现了快速建模。

DEFLECT: Delay-Robust Execution via Flow-matching Likelihood-Estimated Counterfactual Tuning for VLA Policies

Abstract (English): Vision-Language-Action (VLA) policies are typically deployed with asynchronous inference: the robot executes a previously predicted action chunk while the model computes the next one. This creates a prediction-execution misalignment: the chunk is conditioned on the observation taken before inference began, but executes in a physical state that has already drifted forward by several control steps; naive asynchronous rollover collapses from 89% to under 1% on Kinetix as the inference cycle covers up to seven control steps. We introduce DEFLECT, a fully offline post-training refinement that applies as a near drop-in upgrade to existing async-VLA stacks by converting latency itself into a label-free preference signal: counterfactual fresh/stale action pairs are constructed from a frozen reference policy and scored under the deployment-time conditioning via an implicit flow-matching likelihood-ratio surrogate, with no human labels, reward models, or online rollouts. DEFLECT substantially extends the usable delay envelope of async VLA control, with +6.4 success-rate gain in the high-latency regime (5-7 control steps), +4.6 when transferred to a real-scale VLA at the longest delay, and consistent improvements on two real-robot tasks (a bimanual conveyor pick-and-place and a reactive whack-a-mole).

摘要（中文）: 视觉-语言-动作（VLA）策略通常采用异步推理方式部署：机器人执行先前预测的动作片段时，模型同时计算下一个动作片段。这导致了预测与执行之间的错位：动作片段基于推理开始前获取的观测状态进行条件生成，却在实际物理状态已经向前漂移数个控制步后执行；在Kinetix测试中，当推理周期覆盖多达七个控制步时，朴素的异步递推成功率从89%骤降至不足1%。我们提出DEFLECT方法——一种完全离线的训练后优化方案，能够以近乎即插即用的方式升级现有异步VLA系统。该方法将延迟本身转化为无标签的偏好信号：通过冻结参考策略构建反事实的新鲜/陈旧动作对，并利用隐式流匹配似然比代理在部署时条件评分，整个过程无需人工标注、奖励模型或在线试错。DEFLECT显著扩展了异步VLA控制的可容忍延迟范围，在高延迟场景（5-7个控制步）中实现+6.4%的成功率提升，在最长延迟条件下迁移至真实尺度VLA时获得+4.6%的增益，并在两项真实机器人任务（双机械臂传送带分拣作业与反应式打地鼠任务）中均取得持续的性能改进。

TLDR: DEFLECT is a novel, fully offline method for tuning Vision-Language-Action (VLA) policies to be robust to the inherent action-execution delays in asynchronous inference setups, without needing human labels, reward models, or online rollouts.
